## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> What the Catalog Contains

| Data | What it drives |
|---|---|
| **3D model** | Placement in BMR Studio and AR Design |
| **Category** | Which extra data the item needs |
| **Brand, model, SKU** | Which exact product it is |
| **Size and mounting** | Where it can go — floor, wall, ceiling, table, rack, on top of a display, on a mount |
| **Ports** | Connections and cables in Cable mode |
| **Devices** | Camera, microphone, speaker and screen coverage |
| **Acoustic values** | Absorption, for acoustic results and proposals |

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> How the Catalog Is Used

| Where | How it uses the catalog |
|---|---|
| **BMR Studio** | **Equipment** mode has the **Generic**, **Catalog**, **Bundles** <FeatureToggleTag /> and **Branding** tabs. Item data drives coverage overlays and placement. |
| **AR Design** | Place catalog items in the real room with BMR Mobile. |
| **[Room Configurations](../lifecycle/design/default-room-configuration)** | Recommended setups built from catalog items. **Place configuration** drops one into a design. |
| **Cable mode** | Ports — and their **Required** and **PoE** flags — fill the **Connections** panel. Required connections are drawn for you; missing ones show a warning. |
| **Documents** | The room's **Inventory** and **BOM** list catalog items with manufacturer and SKU. |
| **Acoustic optimisation proposal** <FeatureToggleTag /> | Picks acoustic materials from your catalog for a measured room. |
| **Panel murals** | Your own picture printed on acoustic panels that **allow branding**. |

## <Icon name="organization" size="1em" /> Catalogs and Organisations

Catalogs are assigned to an **organisation**. Everyone in it can place items from any of its catalogs.

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Organisation › Preferences › Catalog** | Every catalog your organisation can use — its own, public ones, and ones shared with it — with the number of items. Select one to see what it holds. **View only:** BMR manages the catalogs. |
| **Organisation › Preferences › Acoustic** | **Default acoustics catalog** — the catalog whose acoustic items acoustic measurements and proposals use. |
| **Organisation › Preferences › Bundles** <FeatureToggleTag /> | Reusable kits of catalog items. See [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4). |

Different organisations can have different catalogs, matched to their approved equipment.

## <Icon name="search" size="1em" /> Choosing a Catalog in BMR Studio

**Select a catalog** shows one searchable list:

- **Starred favourites first** — shared catalogs can be starred too.
- Each catalog shows its **owner** and **number of items**.
- **Current** marks the catalog in use. Click another to switch.

## <Icon name="admin-settings" size="1em" /> Managing Catalogs

The **BMR team** handles:

- Adding and removing items
- Uploading and updating 3D models
- Editing item data
- Assigning catalogs to organisations

Need a change? Use **[Send Feedback](../getting-started/send-feedback)** — in the Web header, or under **Settings** in BMR Mobile. See [Requesting a Catalog Item](requesting-items).

Making a 3D model? Follow the [3D Model Standards](3d-model-standards).
