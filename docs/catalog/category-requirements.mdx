# <Icon name="filter" size="1em" /> Requirements by Category

Every item shares the [common backbone](item-anatomy) — name, model, dimensions, image. On top of that, the data an item needs **depends on its category**. A video collaboration device needs ports and placed devices; an acoustic panel needs absorption values; a chair needs none of that.

The catalog has many categories, but they group into a handful of **families** that share a data profile.

---

## <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> Category families

### <Icon name="monitor" size="1em" /> AV & Video Collaboration

Screens, video bars, codecs, cameras, microphones, speakers, control devices, audio/video extenders and converters, and other AV gear.

- **Ports** — the connectors the device exposes, used for cable routing
- **Placed devices** — cameras, microphones, speakers and screens positioned on the 3D model, used for coverage overlays
- Relevant **feature flags** — Screen, Camera, Microphone, Has Speaker, Touch, Is Mount

### <Icon name="acoustics" size="1em" /> Acoustic Treatment

Panels, clouds and bass traps.

- **Absorption values** per octave band — 125 Hz to 8 kHz
- **Per-unit toggle** — interpret values per square metre (default) or per unit (bass traps)
- **Panel width and height** — used for area-based acoustic calculations
- Feature flag — Acoustic

### <Icon name="room" size="1em" /> Furniture & Décor

Chairs, tables, plants, boxes and similar.

- Size, colour, material, image and 3D model
- Mounting — typically Floor or Table
- No ports, no acoustic data

### <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Connectivity & Mounting

Outlets and mounting hardware.

- **Outlets** carry **ports** and appear in the Cable Guide as connection points
- **Mounting hardware** sets the **Is Mount** flag and its compatible surfaces

### <Icon name="copy" size="1em" /> Composite & Unclassified

Bundles group several items together; *Other* and *Uncategorized* are the catch-all while an item is being triaged.

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Requirement matrix

What each family needs on top of the shared backbone. **●** required · **○** optional / when known · **—** not applicable.

| Data | AV / Video | Acoustic | Furniture | Outlets / Mount |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Identity (Name, Display, Brand) | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| **SKU** (component identifier) | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Measurements | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| **Color** | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Material | ○ | ● | ● | ○ |
| Image | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| GLB model | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| USDZ model | ● | ● | ● | ○ |
| Feature flags | ● | ● | ○ | ● |
| Mounting types | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Ports (In / Out) | ● | — | — | ● |
| Placed devices (camera / mic / speaker / screen) | ● | — | — | — |
| Absorption bands (125 Hz – 8 kHz) | — | ● | — | — |
| Panel width × height | — | ● | — | — |

---

:::tip SKU is the key identifier
The **SKU / model number** is how an exact component is identified — always capture it when an item has one. It's the single most valuable field for telling near-identical variants apart.
:::

:::note An evolving profile
As new fields are added to the catalog, they slot into this matrix as new rows — the families stay the same, the data they carry grows over time.
:::
