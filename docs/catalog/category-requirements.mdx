# <Icon name="filter" size="1em" /> Requirements by Category

<PageIntro illustration="catalog" meta={['Reference', 'For suppliers and partners']}>

Every item shares the [common backbone](item-anatomy). On top of that, **its category decides what else it needs**.

</PageIntro>

A video bar needs ports and devices. An acoustic panel needs absorption values. A chair needs neither. The categories group into a few **families**.

---

## <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> Category families

### <Icon name="monitor" size="1em" /> AV & Video Collaboration

Screens, video bars, codecs, cameras, microphones, speakers, control devices, extenders, converters and other AV gear.

- **Ports** — connectors, with direction, **PoE** and **Required** — for connections in Cable mode
- **Placed devices** — camera, microphone, speaker, screen — for coverage overlays
- **Feature flags** — Screen, Touch, Camera, Microphone, Speaker, Control Device

### <Icon name="acoustics" size="1em" /> Acoustic Treatment

Panels, clouds, bass traps and acoustic curtains.

- **Absorption values** per octave band, 125 Hz – 8 kHz
- **Per square metre** (default) or **per unit** (bass traps)
- **Panel width and height**, for area-based calculations — for a curtain, the width is one gather
- **Feature flags** — Acoustic; **Curtain** for curtains; **Allows branding** if a picture can be printed on it

### <Icon name="room" size="1em" /> Furniture & Décor

Chairs, tables, plants, boxes.

- Size, colour, material, image and 3D model
- Mounting — usually Floor or Table
- No ports, no acoustic data

### <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Connectivity & Mounting

Outlets, and mounting hardware such as brackets, stands and camera mounts.

- **Outlets** carry **ports** — the room's connection points in Cable mode
- **Mounts** set **Is Mount** and carry **mounting points** — VESA, Rack or Camera screw, each with an alignment and a capacity. Equipment snaps onto them. See [Mounting points](ports-and-devices#-mounting-points).

### <Icon name="copy" size="1em" /> Composite & Unclassified

*Other* and *Uncategorized* are the catch-all while an item is being sorted.

**Bundles** <FeatureToggleTag /> are not a category: a bundle is a saved set of catalog items, placed as one kit. See [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4).

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Requirement matrix

**●** required · **○** optional / when known · **—** not applicable

| Data | AV / Video | Acoustic | Furniture | Outlets / Mount |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Identity (Name, Display, Brand) | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| **SKU** (component identifier) | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Measurements | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| **Color** | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Material | ○ | ● | ● | ○ |
| Image | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| GLB model | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| USDZ model | ● | ● | ● | ○ |
| Feature flags | ● | ● | ○ | ● |
| Mounting types | ● | ● | ● | ● |
| Ports (In / Out, PoE, Required) | ● | — | — | ● (outlets) |
| Placed devices (camera / mic / speaker / screen) | ● | — | — | — |
| Mounting points (VESA / Rack / Camera screw) | — | — | — | ● (mounts) |
| Absorption bands (125 Hz – 8 kHz) | — | ● | — | — |
| Panel width × height | — | ● | — | — |

---

:::tip SKU is the key identifier
Always send the **SKU / model number** when an item has one. It is what tells near-identical variants apart.
:::

:::note An evolving profile
New fields slot into this matrix as new rows. The families stay the same.
:::
