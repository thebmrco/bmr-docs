# <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Requesting a Catalog Item

The catalog is **managed entirely by the BMR team**. You don't add or edit items yourself — instead you **request** an addition, and BMR builds the complete entry: the data, the 3D model, and any ports or devices.

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> How it works

1. **You request an item** — via **[Send Feedback](../getting-started/send-feedback)** (*Report an Issue* in the Web header, or *Send Feedback* under Settings in BMR Mobile). Tell us the item's name, brand, and what it's for.
2. **BMR gathers the data** — dimensions, materials, features, and any category-specific data such as ports or acoustic values. See [Anatomy of a Catalog Item](item-anatomy) and [Requirements by Category](category-requirements) for what that covers.
3. **BMR prepares the 3D model** — built or sourced and exported to the [3D Model Standards](3d-model-standards), then configured with [ports and devices](ports-and-devices) where relevant.
4. **The item is published** — added to the appropriate catalog and available to place in BMR Studio and AR Design.

---

## <Icon name="info" size="1em" /> What to include in a request

The more you can tell us, the faster the item is ready:

- **Name and brand** of the item
- **Category** it belongs to (display, video bar, acoustic panel, chair, outlet, …)
- A **product link** or datasheet, if you have one
- Where it will be **placed** (floor / wall / ceiling / table)

---

## <Icon name="rotate-3d" size="1em" /> Supplying a 3D model

If you or a manufacturer already have a 3D model, it can be submitted directly — as long as it meets the [3D Model Standards](3d-model-standards). BMR reviews every model against those standards before it's published.

:::info Self-service is on the roadmap
Today all catalog changes go through the BMR team. Self-service catalog management for organisation admins is planned, which will let admins curate their own catalogs directly.
:::
