# <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> The Equipment Page

<PageIntro illustration="catalog" meta={['Web Platform', 'Admins · read only']}>

Every item your organisation can use, in one list. **Look things up here. BMR keeps the items up to date.**

</PageIntro>

Open **Library › Equipment**. The page is for **Admins**. Everyone else meets equipment where they use it: in an Inventory, in a catalog and in BMR Studio.

:::info Read only
You can't add, edit or delete equipment here. **BMR** manages it. See [Requesting a Catalog Item](requesting-items).
:::

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> What the List Shows

An item is on your list when it is:

- **In one of your catalogs.** BMR's catalogs your organisation uses, and catalogs shared with it.
- **Public.** BMR made it visible to every organisation.
- **Yours.** Added in one of your Inventories. See [your organisation's own catalog](index.mdx#your-organisations-own-catalog).

Each row shows:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The item. Click to open it |
| **Brand** | The manufacturer |
| **Owner** | **BETTERMEETINGROOMS** for BMR's items, or the organisation that owns it. A **lock** means **Not public** |
| **Category** | The catalog category |
| **BOM & Inventory category** | Where the item is listed in the BOM and in an Inventory: [Equipment, Infrastructure, Accessories or Cabling](../lifecycle/discovery/inventory#categories) |
| **Features** | What it can do: **Screen**, **Touch**, **Camera**, **Microphone**, **Acoustic** and more |

---

## <Icon name="search" size="1em" /> Search

Type in the search box to find items by name. **Advanced Search** adds filters in three groups:

| Group | Filters |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Name, brand and colour |
| **Categories** | Category and BOM & Inventory category |
| **Features** | Tick what an item must have: **Screen**, **Touch**, **Reflectivity**, **Camera**, **Microphone** or **Acoustic**. A feature you leave unticked means *any* |

---

## <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Open an Item

Click a row for the item's page:

- **Picture and facts**: material, colour, average price and measurements
- **Owner** and **Visibility**: **Public** means every organisation sees it
- **Link** and **description**
- **Catalogs**: which of your catalogs hold the item, and when it was added

Ports, properties and files aren't shown here.

---

## <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Something Wrong or Missing?

Only BMR changes equipment. Use **[Send Feedback](../getting-started/send-feedback)** with the item's **name**, **brand** and **SKU**. Need a new item? See [Requesting a Catalog Item](requesting-items).

Your organisation's **Bundles** <FeatureToggleTag /> have their own tab on this page.
