# <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Ports, Devices & Configurator

<PageIntro illustration="catalog" meta={['Reference', 'Set up by BMR']}>

A model alone isn't enough. BMR also needs **where the connectors are**, **where the active parts sit** and **where other equipment attaches**.

</PageIntro>

The **3D Equipment Configurator** records three things on the item's model:

| On the model | Drives |
|---|---|
| **Ports** | Connections and cables in Cable mode |
| **Placed devices** | Coverage overlays in BMR Studio |
| **Mounting points** | Equipment snapping onto a mount |

---

## <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Ports

A **port** is a physical connector on the item.

| Attribute | Values |
|---|---|
| **Name** | A label, e.g. *HDMI 1*, *Power 1* |
| **Type** | Ethernet · Power · USB-C · USB-A · USB-B · HDMI · Analog-Audio · Optical · GPIO · RS232 · Custom |
| **Direction** | **In**, **Out**, or both |
| **PoE** | The port carries **Power over Ethernet** — supplied by a device with its own power (a codec, a switch), received by one without (a microphone, a camera) |
| **Required** | It must be connected. Cable mode proposes and checks it. A power input is required by default |

Ports fill the **Connections** panel in Cable mode: required connections are drawn for you, and a missing one shows a warning on the device, such as *Power missing* or *PoE missing*. **Outlets** have ports too — they are the room's connection points, and Ethernet always runs through one.

---

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Placed devices

A **device** is an active part positioned on the model. Each one drives a coverage overlay in BMR Studio:

| Device | Overlay | Data from the datasheet |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | Field of view | Horizontal field of view at 1× zoom, zoom, min and max distance |
| **Microphone** | Pickup range | Pickup pattern, radius |
| **Speaker** | Coverage area | Dispersion, horizontal and vertical angle, distance |
| **Screen** | Visibility zone | Screen size (inches), aspect ratio |

## <Icon name="marker" size="1em" /> Mounting points

A **mount** (an item with **Is Mount**) carries one or more **mounting points**: where equipment attaches, and which way it faces.

| Attribute | Values |
|---|---|
| **Type** | **VESA** (displays) · **Rack** (rack-mount equipment) · **Camera screw** (¼"-20) |
| **Alignment** | Centred on the point · above it (bottom on the point) · below it (top on the point) |
| **Capacity** | How many items the point can hold |

In BMR Studio, equipment with a matching mounting type **snaps onto the point** and moves with the mount. A mount without mounting points holds equipment on top of it.

---

## <Icon name="rotate-3d" size="1em" /> How it's configured

Pick **Place Port** or **Place Device** (camera, microphone, speaker, screen or mounting point), then click the spot on the model. Each placement is saved with the item, so every copy in every room has the same connectors, coverage and mounting points.

:::info Set up by BMR
The **BMR team** configures ports, devices and mounting points when the item is added. **Ports verified** marks an item whose port positions have been checked against the real device.
:::
