# <Icon name="marker" size="1em" /> Markers

<PageIntro illustration="markers" meta={['BMR Mobile', 'During the scan']}>

A **marker** is a photo pinned to its exact spot in the 3D scan.
**The scan shows the room's shape. Markers show what is in it.**

</PageIntro>

:::important Markers are a core part of every room
No markers: no inventory, and whoever designs or installs the room has to guess. Take them every time you scan.
:::

## <Icon name="marker" size="1em" /> What to capture {#-what-to-mark}

Tap **Tips for markers** during the scan. Shoot each item, then tick its row.

<div style={{display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'minmax(0, 1fr) auto', gap: '1.5rem', alignItems: 'center'}} className="markers-capture">

<div>

| Capture | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Power outlets** | Incl. floor boxes and behind furniture |
| **Network points** | Data, ethernet, wall plates |
| **Existing AV** | Cameras, screens, speakers, codecs |
| **Walls** | One overview each |
| **Ceiling** | One overview |
| **Anything unusual** | Cable trays, pillars, glass walls, vents, light switches |

**At least 10 markers** per room. Not sure? **Mark it.** It takes seconds; a missing one can cost a site visit.

</div>

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> When to capture {#-when-to-capture}

| | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ **Best** | **During the scan**: tap the camera button as you pass each item | It lands in the right spot, with no extra work |
| 👍 **OK** | Right after the scan, **before you leave** | Still in the room, still fresh |
| ⚠️ **Avoid** | After you've left | A second trip, and things get missed |

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Markers without a scan {#-markers-and-the-room-scan}

A marker only lands in 3D if the app knows where you stand.

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| **During the scan** | Placed on the scan. Nothing extra to do |
| **Later, room already scanned** | Turn round the room until **Room recognised**, then capture. See [AR World Map](../../technology/ar-world-map#-if-it-still-cant-find-the-room) |
| **Recognition skipped** | Saved as a photo, without a position |
| **No scan yet** | Saved as a photo only. Use **Scan & Add Markers** to get both |

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> Where markers are used {#-where-markers-show-up}

| Where | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Room › Markers** (app and web) | Every marker, with its photo |
| **[Inventory](./inventory)** | Close-ups turned into a list of what is in the room |
| **BMR Studio** | Each marker on the 3D scan, where it was taken. **Markers** menu › **Show markers** / **View All** |
| **Data Completeness** | **Markers Captured** on the location and organisation overview |
