# <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Room Scan

The scan is the **first thing you do in a room** and the foundation for everything else. Using the LiDAR sensor in an iPhone or iPad, BMR Mobile builds a 3D model of the space in a few minutes.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> What the scan captures

| Captured | Used for |
|---|---|
| **3D geometry** (walls, floor, ceiling, openings) | The room model in BMR Studio; room volume for acoustic calculations |
| **Compass orientation** | Referencing walls by direction — see [Compass](./compass) |
| **AR world map** | Recognising the room on later visits, so markers, acoustic measurements and AR designs land in their true positions |

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> What the scan unlocks

Without a scan, the room works in a limited way:

- **Markers** are stored as photos only, without coordinates.
- **Acoustic measurements** run in the Basic flow — no speaker or measurement positions.
- **AR Design** can be explored but designs cannot be saved.

This is why the app's guidance starts with **"Start here — Scan the room"**, and why scanning and marker capture are best done in the same visit.

## <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> What you need

- An iPhone or iPad with **LiDAR** — iPhone 12 Pro or newer, iPad Pro (2020) or newer
- **iOS 26** or newer
- Reasonable lighting — LiDAR works in the dark, but the camera needs light to texture the scan
