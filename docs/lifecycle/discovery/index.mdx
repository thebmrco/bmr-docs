# <Icon name="search" size="1em" /> Discovery

<PageIntro illustration="scan">

Using just an iPhone, you can perform a guided acoustic measurement grounded in peer-reviewed research — results you can compare between rooms and over time, with no specialist equipment. The same workflow captures a 3D model of the space with your iPhone or iPad and documents key room elements.

</PageIntro>

**Discovery** is the first step in the BETTERMEETINGROOMS lifecycle. The data you capture is stored in the room's **Room Data** and is the foundation for **Design** and **Maintenance**.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Room Data

All data collected during Discovery is stored in the **Room Data** section of the room in the app:

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| 3D scan geometry | Room Scan |
| Compass orientation | Room Scan |
| Markers & photos | Marker placement |
| Inventory | Built from the close-up markers ([Inventory](./inventory)) |
| Acoustic profile | Acoustic Measurement |

The room's **compass orientation** is automatically captured during the scan and synced to the backend. This allows the room to be displayed in its true geographic orientation in BMR Studio — useful for referencing walls by direction (e.g. "the screen is on the west wall").

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Prerequisites & Tips

- ✅ iPhone or iPad with **LiDAR** — see [System Requirements](../../getting-started/system-requirements#-iphone--ipad)
- ✅ Good lighting helps the scan — avoid scanning in very dark rooms
- ✅ Scan the room **before** capturing markers or measuring — without a scan, markers have no coordinates and the acoustic measurement gets no MOS score ([why](./room-scan#what-the-scan-unlocks))
- ✅ Run the acoustic measurement **before** requesting a design — placement decisions improve significantly

> The more complete the Discovery data, the faster and better the design will be.
