## <Icon name="users" size="1em" /> Ask someone to take a marker {#assign-a-marker-task}

No photo confirms an item yet? Send someone to take one. The item is **verified when the marker is taken**.

1. Click the item, then **Assign a marker task**.
2. Under **Assign to**, pick a person. The list holds everyone with access to the room: admins, location managers and room managers. Search by name.
3. Click **Assign**.

The task lands in their BMR app, under [My Tasks](../../getting-started/my-tasks). The item shows **Marker task assigned** and is verified as soon as the marker is in. Changed your mind? **Cancel task** puts it back to not checked.

## <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> The Four Categories {#categories}

Every item belongs to one of four categories. The category decides where the item is listed in an Inventory and in the BOM.

| Category | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Equipment** | Anything in the room that is relatively simple to replace, like a room bar, display, chair or table |
| **Infrastructure** | Things that are more fixed in the room, like an air conditioning unit, power outlet or fixed control panel |
| **Accessories** | Anything that is neither Equipment nor Infrastructure, normally there to make the room look better, like a plant, picture or laptop |
| **Cabling** | All cables, like HDMI and network cables |

In the Inventory, **Accessories** and **Cabling** start closed: click the heading to open one. The **Category** column is something else, the catalog category, like Speakers or Outlets.

Marked a window as a **Room feature** in BMR Mobile? It is listed under **Infrastructure**.
