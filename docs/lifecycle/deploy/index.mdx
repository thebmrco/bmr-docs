## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> The Active Design

A room can have many designs. **One** can be Active: the design the room is built to.

- The record of what is installed, where, and how it is connected.
- The starting point for the next change.
- Every other design is a **proposal**, or **was active** before.

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Deployment outputs

| Output | Where |
|---|---|
| **Bill of materials** — equipment and cables, by type and length | Room › **Documents** › **BOM** · Studio **BOM** tab |
| **Cable plans** | **Plans** |
| **Versions** of the design | Studio **Versions** tab |

The BOM can be **downloaded**, and two designs or versions **compared**.

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> Marking a design as Active

When the room is installed:

1. Open the room's **Designs** tab.
2. **Activate** the design that matches the room. In BMR Studio: **Set as active design**.
3. On-site, check the room against it, then **Mark verified** on the active design.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Activate** | Makes this design the Active Design. The previous one is replaced. |
| **Delete** | Not possible for the Active Design: activate another one first. |
| **Activation History** | Who activated which design, and when. |
| **Mark verified** | Confirms the room was checked against this design. Click again to undo. |

The next change to the room starts as a new design; activate it once it is built.

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> What the Active Design enables

- **Maintenance** — see what is installed and how it is connected.
- **Upgrades** — build the next design on top of it.
- **Portfolio view** — the active state of every room in the organisation.
