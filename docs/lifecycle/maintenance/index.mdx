# <Icon name="wrench" size="1em" /> Maintenance

<PageIntro illustration="room-state" meta={['Web platform']}>

Keep rooms working after installation. Everyone works from **one live record** of the room — what is there, where, and how it connects.

</PageIntro>

---

## Current scope {#current-scope}

Maintenance builds on what Discovery and Design captured. Today you use these, all on the [Room page](../../structure/room#-the-room-page):

| Tool | Where | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> **Active Design** | **Designs** tab | The record of the room as installed — equipment, layout, cabling |
| <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> **Mark verified** | **Designs** tab, on the active design | Confirms the room was checked against the design |
| <Icon name="list-checks" size="1em" /> **Inventory** | **Documents** tab | What is in the room today, built from the markers and kept as versions — see [Room Inventory](../discovery/inventory) |
| <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> **BOM** | **Documents** tab | What the active design needs, cables included — compare versions or designs |
| <Icon name="activity-log" size="1em" /> **Room history** | **Room Data** tab | A timeline of every scan, marker, measurement and design. **Actives** shows what is current; tick two scans or measurements to compare them |
| <Icon name="audio-lines" size="1em" /> **Acoustic measurements** | **Room Data** tab | The measured score and the recommendations |

**IT, facilities and support teams** all work from the same record, without manual upkeep.

## Roadmap {#roadmap}

Planned for Maintenance:

- **Live integration** with external platforms
- **Automated status monitoring** of installed devices
- **Troubleshooting workflows** linked to the room's Active Design
- **Upgrade requests** that start a new Design cycle
- **Lifecycle analytics** on performance and usage over time

---

## Outputs of Maintenance {#outputs-of-maintenance}

- A central **room record**, with all data in one place
- Faster **troubleshooting** and less downtime
- A clear path to **room upgrades**
