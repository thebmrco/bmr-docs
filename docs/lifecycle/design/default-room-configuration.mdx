## <Icon name="admin-settings" size="1em" /> Part 1 — Setting up a configuration

Organisation › **Room Configurations**. Three tabs:

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Yours** | Your organisation's own setups. Designers see these **first**. |
| **Shared with you** | Setups other organisations shared with yours. Place them as they are, or save a copy to adapt. They can't be changed. |
| **BMR Defaults** | Complete setups maintained by BMR, offered to every organisation. Check which sizes they cover, and add your own for the rest. |

**Add a room configuration** — **Start from scratch**, or **Start from an existing one** (yours or a BMR Default) to copy and change it.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Shown in BMR Studio's Room Configuration tab. |
| **Size range (m²)** | *Studio offers it for rooms of* — the rooms it applies to. |
| **For a group of** *(optional)* | How many people it is meant for. |

A configuration holds two kinds of equipment:

| Part | Built | In the design |
|---|---|---|
| **Placed** | **Edit in BMR Studio**, in a sample room of this size | Placed in one go — walls, floor, ceiling |
| **Unplaced** | **Add items** from a list — can include kits | A list the designer places item by item |

:::danger The size range decides where it is offered
A configuration is offered to a room only when the room's **floor area** falls within the range. A room with **no floor area set** matches none. Floor area comes from the scan. Designers can still pick any configuration with **Browse all**.
:::

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> Part 2 — Placing the equipment in BMR Studio

Open the **Room Configuration** tab in **Equipment** mode. It was called **Guidelines** before the November 2026 release. A badge on the tab counts the categories the design already meets: **4/7** is four of seven. A new design from a scan also offers the configurations first, in the [welcome dialog](bmr-studio#starting-a-design).

1. **Pick a configuration.** A card shows the name, whose it is (**BMR** or **Custom**) and how many items it holds. The first match is selected for you.
2. **Place configuration.** The placed layout goes onto the wall it fits best, or into the middle of the room when nothing of it hangs on a wall. [What happens](#placing-the-configuration).
3. **Fill the gaps.** Open a category to see its items. The missing ones have **Place in room**, and so do the **Suggested kits**.

**Browse all** also shows the configurations outside the room's size, marked **Doesn't fit this room's size**. **Matching only** goes back. If nothing matches, the tab says **No room configurations match this room's size**: the floor area is outside every range, or not set.

The status shows how the design compares. Each category has the same three states, with its count (*in the design / asked for*):

| Status | Colour | Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Following the room configuration** | Green | Every category met, with the configuration's own equipment. |
| **Quantities met, different equipment** | Amber | Right counts, other models. |
| **Below the room configuration** | Red | Something is missing. |

Only items from a catalog count. **Chairs 10 / 4** in amber means ten chairs where four are asked for, none of them the configuration's own picks.

It's a starting point, not a lock — change anything you like.

### What Place configuration does {#placing-the-configuration}

- **It picks the wall.** Each outer wall is checked for the layout's width, the room in front of it, and clear space around doors and windows. The best fit wins. A layout with nothing on the walls goes to the middle of the room.
- **It asks first if something is in the way.** The Studio lists the items. **Remove them and place** clears them. **Place anyway** goes ahead, and what overlaps is marked.
- **It places one group**, named after the configuration. While it is selected, the other walls it fits on light up. Click one, or pick it under **Move to another wall** in the **Properties** tab, to move it there. One undo brings it back.
- **It marks what doesn't fit.** An item that doesn't fit is labelled **Does not fit**, in the room and in the [BOM](bmr-studio#bom), with the reason. This is saved with the design and checked again after every change.

Rooms differ in shape. Look over what lands on the other walls.

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Why it matters

- **Same standard everywhere** — every room of a size gets the same recommendation.
- **Faster design** — start from a full layout, not an empty room.
- **Predictable equipment** — easier procurement and support.

A **<U m2={30} />** room gets **Medium Meeting**. If ranges overlap, all matches are listed: **yours first, then the ones shared with you, then BMR Defaults**, each by name.

## <Icon name="edit" size="1em" /> Managing configurations

On the **Yours** tab, open a configuration to:

- **Edit** the name, size, people range or equipment.
- **Delete** it, from the **⋮** menu — removed for every location. Designs that already used it keep their equipment.

**Shared with you** and **BMR Defaults** can't be changed. Open one and choose **Save as new room configuration** for a copy of your own.
