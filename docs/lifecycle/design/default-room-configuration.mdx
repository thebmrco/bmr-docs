## <Icon name="admin-settings" size="1em" /> Part 1 — Setting up a configuration

Organisation › **Room Configurations**. Two tabs:

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Yours** | Your organisation's own setups. Designers see these **first**. |
| **BMR Defaults** | Complete setups maintained by BMR, offered to every organisation. |

**Add a room configuration** — **Start from scratch**, or **Start from an existing one** (yours or a BMR Default) to copy and change it.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Shown in BMR Studio's Guidelines. |
| **Size range (m²)** | *Studio offers it for rooms of* — the rooms it applies to. |
| **For a group of** *(optional)* | How many people it is meant for. |

A configuration holds two kinds of equipment:

| Part | Built | In the design |
|---|---|---|
| **Placed** | **Edit in BMR Studio**, in a sample room of this size | Placed in one go — walls, floor, ceiling |
| **Unplaced** | **Add items** from a list — can include kits | A list the designer places item by item |

:::danger The size range decides where it is offered
A configuration is offered only when the room's **floor area** falls within the range. A room with **no floor area set** gets no configuration. Floor area comes from the scan.
:::

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> Part 2 — Placing the equipment in BMR Studio

Open the **Guidelines** tab in BMR Studio.

1. **Pick a configuration** — the first match is selected for you.
2. **Place configuration** — the whole placed layout lands on the wall you point at. Press **R** to turn it to the next wall. Check items afterwards: rooms differ in shape.
3. **Fill the gaps** — missing categories have **Place in room**.

The status shows how the design compares:

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| **Following guidelines** | All categories met. |
| **Quantities met, different equipment** | Right counts, other models. |
| **Below guidelines** | Something is missing. |

It's a starting point, not a lock — change anything you like.

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Why it matters

- **Same standard everywhere** — every room of a size gets the same recommendation.
- **Faster design** — start from a full layout, not an empty room.
- **Predictable equipment** — easier procurement and support.

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Example

| Configuration | Size range | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| Small Huddle | <U m2={[10,20]} /> | Room Bar, 55" display, 4 chairs, table |
| Medium Meeting | <U m2={[20,40]} /> | Room Bar, dual 65" displays, 6 chairs, table |
| Large Boardroom | <U m2={[40,80]} /> | Codec, 3 ceiling mics, dual 75" displays, 14 chairs, table |

A **<U m2={30} />** room gets **Medium Meeting**. If ranges overlap, all matches are listed: **yours first, then BMR Defaults**, each by name.

## <Icon name="edit" size="1em" /> Managing configurations

On the **Yours** tab:

- **Edit** the size, people range or equipment.
- **Save as new room configuration** to branch off a copy.
- **Delete** — removed for every location. Designs that already used it keep their equipment.
