## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Modes: how the Studio works {#-modes}

Every job has its own **mode**. Switch at the bottom of the screen. The mode decides:

- **what you can select and change.** Everything else stays visible, but locked.
- **which tabs the sidebar shows**, and which tools you get.

<StudioModes />

A design opens in **View**. **Versions** and **BOM** are in the sidebar in the four main modes. The first time you open each mode, a short **guided tour** shows you around.

## <Icon name="eye" size="1em" /> View {#view}

Look around and present. Nothing can move, so it's safe for reviews. Select anything to read its properties.

- **View menu** (the eye): **2D / 3D**, **In room** (walk through at eye height), **Coverage** and **Layers** (dimensions, labels, cables …).
- **Markers** from the scan: their own button in the toolbar.
- **Presentation tab:** save views to [present](#presenting-a-design).

### Render modes {#render-modes}

In the toolbar, in **View** mode.

Finer settings: **Room** tab › **Rendering**. The 3D view uses **WebGPU** where the browser supports it — see [System Requirements](../../getting-started/system-requirements).

#### Photoreal <FeatureToggleTag /> {#photoreal}

<FeatureToggle />

A third render mode: a **path-traced**, photo-like picture. It refines while the camera is still (*Rendering 12 / 64*), then denoises.

- Needs **WebGPU** and the **3D** view (not the floor plan).
- Passes and denoising: **Room** tab › **Rendering**.

## <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> Equipment {#equipment}

Place and arrange equipment, furniture, outlets, logos and murals. The **Guidelines** tab shows your organisation's setup for this room, see [Default Room Configuration](default-room-configuration).

### Equipment tabs {#equipment-tabs}

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Generic** | Placeholder items — a display, a chair — when the model doesn't matter yet. |
| **Catalog** | Your organisation's [Equipment Catalog](../../catalog/). |
| **Bundles** <FeatureToggleTag /> | Saved sets of equipment, placed in one go. |
| **Branding** | Your logos and [panel murals](#panel-murals). |

Equipment **snaps onto mounts** (VESA, rack, camera screw) and moves with them. Drag it off to detach.

### Bundles <FeatureToggleTag /> {#bundles}

<FeatureToggle />

A **bundle** is a reusable kit — for example a video bar, display, mount and table microphone.

1. Select **at least two** items in the room.
2. Choose **Create bundle**, name it, tick the **catalogs** and what it **Mounts to**.
3. **Save bundle**. Place it from the **Bundles** tab.

The placed copy is independent: editing the bundle later doesn't change rooms that already use it.

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-bundle-editor.jpg"
  alt="A bundle open in BMR Studio in its own small room — one wall with the floor in front of it — holding two displays, a video bar and a stand, with the Equipment sidebar showing the Generic, Catalog, Bundles and Branding tabs"
  caption="A bundle opens in its own one-wall room. Note the four Equipment tabs on the right."
/>

### Panel murals {#panel-murals}

A panel mural is your own picture printed across a group of acoustic panels. Create it under **Organisation › Preferences › Branding**, then place it from the **Branding** tab like any other item. It shows in the BOM as **Custom print**.

## <Icon name="cable" size="1em" /> Cables {#cable-mode}

Cabling starts from **what needs to be connected**, not from drawing cables.

Here you change cables, outlets and boxes. Other equipment stays put: click a device to see what's plugged in.

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-cable-connections-3d.jpg"
  alt="Cable mode in BMR Studio: the Connections panel on the left lists each device's ports with the connected port, cable type filters at the top and short warnings such as no free port, next to a see-through 3D room with the cables drawn in colour"
  caption="The Connections panel, next to the see-through 3D room."
/>

- **Connections panel** — each device lists its connections, **required** ones first. Pick a port, **Connect to another port**, or delete a cable. **Reset connections** starts over.
- **Required connections are drawn for you** when you open Cable mode. Ethernet runs through an outlet; **PoE** is supported.
- **Warnings sit on the device** — *Power missing*, *PoE missing*, *no free port* — with the fix one click away.
- **Filter by cable type** — Power, HDMI, Network, USB, Other — each with its own colour, also in **Plans**.
- **Cables save as you go.**

**2D, Split or 3D.** The 2D view is the **unfolded room**: walls, floor and ceiling laid out flat. Select a cable to reshape it on the grid; move sockets and equipment along guides (arrow keys nudge).

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-cable-split-view.jpg"
  alt="Cable mode in Split view: the unfolded room on the left, with the walls laid out in a row between the ceiling and the floor and the cables drawn on them, and the same room in 3D on the right"
  caption="Split view: the unfolded room next to 3D."
/>

## <Icon name="audio-lines" size="1em" /> Acoustics <FeatureToggleTag /> {#acoustics}

<FeatureToggle />

Recommends absorbers for the room and simulates the result against the **DIN 18041** target. You choose where treatment may go, then place and move the absorbers. Built on the room's [acoustic measurement](../../acoustics/acoustics-guide).

## <Icon name="copy" size="1em" /> Design versions

A room can have several **designs**, and each design keeps its own **versions**.

### Save and Save as New Design {#automatic-versioning}

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Saves your changes as a **new version** (*Version 2*, *Version 3* …). Earlier versions are kept. |
| **Save as New Design** | Makes a **separate design**, not linked to this one. |

- **Switching mode saves too**, as a new version, if you have unsaved changes.
- Cable changes **save automatically**.
- If a save fails, BMR Studio says why and offers **Retry**. Unsaved changes from an earlier session can be **restored** when you reopen the design.
- The **Versions** tab lists every version: **Activate** one to make it current, **Rename** or **Delete** the others.

## <Icon name="monitor" size="1em" /> Presenting a design {#presenting-a-design}

**Present** (Shift + F), next to the modes, takes the design full screen and steps through it one view at a time.

- **Standard views** first (Overview, Floor plan, …), then **your saved views**.
- **← / →** to step, jump to any view from the bar, **take a screenshot**, **Esc** to stop.
- People you share a design with read-only can present it too.

### Saving your own views

In **View** mode, open the **Presentation** tab, set up the camera and click **Save current view**. Views can be renamed, reordered, replaced or removed.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Reviewing and activating a design {#-reviewing-and-approving-a-design}

The same for Studio designs and AR Designs:

1. Open the room's **Designs** tab.
2. Check the 3D layout, the cables and the **BOM**.
3. When the room is built to it, **Activate** the design — or **Set as active design** in BMR Studio's **Room** tab.

The Active Design is the room's record. See [Deploy](../deploy/).

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> How to open BMR Studio

### On the web

1. Open the room at **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**.
2. Go to the **Designs** tab and pick a design — or create one with **New Design**. It opens in BMR Studio, full screen.

The room's Overview also links to **the active design in the Studio**.

### On iPhone or iPad

1. Open the room in BMR Mobile.
2. Go to **Designs** and open a design.

BMR Mobile opens existing designs. To start a new one in the app, use [AR Design](ar-design-guide).

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Tips

- **Can't select something?** It's in another mode. Tap **Switch**.
- **Review in View**: nothing moves by accident.
- **Start from a scan**: a good scan means less fixing in Structurals.
- **Every Save is a version**: go back from the **Versions** tab.
- **Save your views before a meeting**, then present with **Shift + F**.
