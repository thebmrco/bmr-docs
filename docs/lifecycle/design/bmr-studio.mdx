# <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> BMR Studio

**BMR Studio** is the single 3D environment used for all room design work in BMR. It is designed to be used **on the web, on a computer** — that is where detailed design work belongs. It also runs inside **BMR Mobile** on **iPad** and **iPhone** for reviewing designs on-site. BMR Studio is where you model rooms, place equipment, manage design versions, and mark a design as **Active**.

:::info Beta feature — available to all users
BMR Studio is currently in **Beta**, but enabled for everyone. A full public release is coming soon.
:::

## <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> BMR Studio on iPad and iPhone

BMR Studio also runs inside **BMR Mobile** on iPad and iPhone, so you can open and review the same 3D design environment while on-site. Each device fits a different use case:

- **Web (on a computer)** — the primary design environment, with the screen space and precise pointer that detailed design needs. **Recommended for all real design work.**
- **iPad** — it runs, but is **not recommended** for detailed design: the touch interface and smaller screen make precise editing harder. Best for **reviewing** a design on-site.
- **iPhone** — **preview only**. The screen is too small for editing and the interface is not optimised for it. Use the iPhone to walk a room while reviewing a design, then switch to a computer for any actual changes.

While viewing a design in BMR Mobile you can switch between **2D** and **3D** views and toggle **Measurements**, **Cables**, **POIs**, and the **Legend** overlays — the same controls as on the Web.

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Modes

BMR Studio uses dedicated modes so you can focus on one thing at a time:

- **View** — explore the room and review designs. Navigate the 3D model, inspect placed equipment, and get an overview of the current design state.
- **Structurals** — adjust structural components such as walls, windows, doors, and other building elements. **Windows and doors are shown by default** when you enter the mode, room resizing is smoother, and **surface materials and colours from the scan are applied to the generated design** — so the model more closely resembles the real room.
- **Equipment** — add and remove equipment and furniture in the design. Browse the full catalog, place items, configure quantities, and position everything in the room. Recommended items from a matching [Default Room Configuration](default-room-configuration) can be placed straight from the **Guidelines** panel.
- **Cables** — plan and document cable routing between devices. **Outlets are shown in the Cable Guide** for a complete picture of the room's connection points, and two BETA tools speed up routing: **Auto-Route** bundles cables that share a path, branching off only at the final drop, and **Tidy Bundles** cleans up existing routing.

More modes will be added over time, and existing modes will continue to be refined.

## <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> What You Can Do

- **Place and configure equipment** from the catalog — cameras, screens, speakers, codecs, furniture, and more. Equipment **stays selected while you rotate it**, and newly placed devices are **auto-selected** with their **Advanced properties** shown by default.
- **Adjust room structure** — walls, windows, doors, and other building elements, with surface materials applied from the scan
- **Plan cable routing** between devices, including outlets and auto-routing
- **Work with automatic design versions** for the same room — every save creates a version you can review, rename, activate, or delete
- **Generate an equipment list** automatically from placed items
- **Move a design through approval** and mark it as Active when installed
- **Follow Default Room Configuration guidelines** — when a room matches a defined size range, the **Guidelines** panel shows fulfilled and missing categories with a one-click **Place in room** action

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> How to Open BMR Studio

### On the Web

1. Go to **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**
2. Open the room
3. Navigate to **Design**
4. Click **Open Studio** — the 3D editor loads with your room scan as the base

### On iPhone or iPad

1. Open the room in BMR Mobile
2. Go to the **Designs** tab
3. Open an existing design or create a new one — BMR Studio loads inside the app

## <Icon name="copy" size="1em" /> Design Versions

Each room can have **multiple design versions** in BMR Studio, and your work is **never overwritten**. This lets you:

- Explore different equipment configurations or layout options side by side
- Preserve historical versions as the room evolves
- Track which version was actually deployed

### Automatic versioning

Versions are **created automatically** whenever you save or change mode — you do not have to remember to snapshot your work. Every previous version can be reviewed, **renamed**, **activated**, or **deleted** at any time from the **Versions** tab.

- **Save as New Design** — in the top menu, for when you want a separate design rather than another version of the current one.
- **Designs are shown as a list** in the room design view, making it easier to identify and open the right one.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Reviewing and Approving a Design

Whether created in BMR Studio, via AR Design, or delivered by a BMR designer, the review process is the same:

1. Open the room's **Design** section to view available versions
2. Inspect the 3D layout, coverage visuals, and equipment list
3. Add comments or request adjustments
4. **Approve** the design when ready
5. Once physically installed, **mark it as Active** — it becomes the Active Design and the room's live record

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Tips

:::tip
The richer your Discovery data — scan quality, marker coverage, acoustic measurements — the more accurately BMR Studio reflects the real room.
:::

- **Start with a scan** — BMR Studio uses the 3D scan as the foundation. A high-quality scan means more accurate placement.
- **Use modes** — switch between modes rather than trying to do everything at once. Each mode surfaces the tools relevant to that task.
- **Use versions freely** — every save is captured automatically, so you can explore options without fear of losing earlier work. Use **Save as New Design** when you want a separate design rather than another version.
- **Check Default Room Configurations** — if your organisation has defined recommended setups, they appear automatically in the sidebar when the room size matches.
