## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Modes: how the Studio works {#-modes}

Every job has its own **mode**. Switch at the bottom of the screen. The mode decides:

- **what you can select and change.** Everything else stays visible, but locked.
- **which tabs the sidebar shows**, and which tools you get.

## <Icon name="eye" size="1em" /> View {#view}

Look around and present. Nothing can move, so it's safe for reviews. Select anything to read its properties.

The controls for looking sit together in the middle of the toolbar, in every mode:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **2D · Split · 3D** | The floor plan, the 3D room, or both side by side. **Split** needs a wide screen and is in Cables mode for now. |
| <Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> **View settings** | What the room shows, and how it is lit. See [View settings](#view-settings). |
| <Icon name="radar" size="1em" /> **Coverage** | What the cameras, microphones, displays and loudspeakers reach. See [Coverage](#coverage). |
| <Icon name="video" size="1em" /> **Camera** | Preset views (**Overview**, **Top**, four **Sides**) and your saved ones. **In room** (View mode) walks the room at eye height, **Camera view** sees what a device camera sees, **Center camera on object** frames your selection. |

Next to them: **Render mode** (View mode only, see [Render modes](#render-modes)), **Measure**, and **Markers** from the scan once the room has some. The **Presentation tab** saves views to [present](#presenting-a-design).

### View settings {#view-settings}

<Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> opens **Space Configuration** beside the 3D view. Every switch applies at once, and each section folds away.

| Section | What you switch |
|---|---|
| **Dimensions & guides** | Wall dimensions, measurements, guides, placement warnings |
| **People** | Mannequins on the chairs: who they are, and how many chairs are taken |
| **Room** | Doors & windows, wall transparency, interior wall transparency |
| **Equipment** | Cables, ceiling equipment, acoustic treatment, equipment labels |
| **Collisions**, **Grid** | In the editing modes: what stops a move, and the grid items snap to |
| **Ceiling grid** | The suspended ceiling's tile grid: set up in the editing modes, shown or hidden anywhere |
| **Lighting & environment** | Lighting, background colour and the render settings. See [Render modes](#render-modes) |

Switches appear only when the room has something to switch: no acoustic treatment, no switch for it.

### Coverage {#coverage}

<Icon name="radar" size="1em" /> shows what the devices in the design reach, one type at a time: **None** (the default), **Display**, **Microphone**, **Speaker** <FeatureToggleTag /> or **Camera**.

| Pick | What it draws |
|---|---|
| **Display** | Where the screen stays legible for detailed content, from the AVIXA DISCAS viewing-distance rule |
| **Microphone** | How far each microphone picks up speech at full level, and where it starts to fall off |
| **Speaker** <FeatureToggleTag /> | Which chairs the loudspeakers reach evenly. See [Loudspeaker coverage](#speaker-coverage) |
| **Camera** | Each camera's field of view. What is inside it is framed at the far end |

- The coverage is named on the picture, with an **i** that explains it in a few lines.
- A type the design has no device of is greyed out: **None in this design**.
- In **In room** and **Camera view** the menu is off, because coverage isn't drawn there.
- Ceiling items that aren't part of the coverage (lights, projectors, ceiling acoustic panels, the suspended ceiling) turn see-through. The coverage's own devices stay in view, highlighted.

<Notice kind="simulation" />

#### Loudspeaker coverage <FeatureToggleTag /> {#speaker-coverage}

<FeatureToggle />

Do the chairs hear the loudspeakers evenly? Each chair is rated against the best-served chair, on direct sound only (no reflections), at 4 kHz.

- **Green**: within 6 dB of the best chair. Speech is clear there.
- **Red**: more than 6 dB below it. Speech is harder to understand and listening takes more effort.

The bar at the bottom says **Recommended coverage** and counts the chairs: *n covered*, *n not covered*. The floor shows where the sound is within 6 dB of the best chair. Without chairs, the area is drawn from the loudest place in the room.

**Details** opens more:

| Detail | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Frequency** | **1 kHz** or **4 kHz** (the default). Most loudspeakers get narrower as the frequency rises. |
| **Rings** | Lines at 3, 6, 9 and 12 dB below the best chair |
| **Evenness** | Loudest to quietest chair: **very even** (up to 3 dB), **even** (up to 6), **noticeably uneven** (up to 8), **uneven** (up to 12) or **very uneven** (more). The classes are the coverage envelope of AVIXA A102.01. |
| **Data** | Where each loudspeaker's pattern comes from: **manufacturer**, **estimated** or **none, 90° assumed**. Shown when a loudspeaker has no manufacturer data. |

**manufacturer** is the directivity from the datasheet, which BMR adds for some loudspeakers. **estimated** comes from the coverage angle in the catalog item. With **none**, a 90° angle is assumed. For a better picture, keep the coverage angle filled in on your [catalog items](../../catalog/ports-and-devices#placed-devices).

**Photoreal** <FeatureToggleTag /> is a third mode, for photo-like pictures. See [Photoreal rendering](#photoreal) below.

### BOM {#bom}

In **View** and **Equipment** the **BOM** opens in a panel on the left. It lists **every item in the room**, from a catalog or not: picture, name, category and maker, SKU and quantity, and the ceiling's materials. Cables are in it too.

- A line with no catalog model says **Not in catalog**, adds **Detected** if the scan found it, and gives its size.
- **Download as PDF** or **Excel** from the panel's header. Not-in-catalog lines are included.
- **Hide BOM** closes the panel, **Show BOM** on the slim rail brings it back. Drag its edge to resize it.

## <Icon name="photo" size="1em" /> Photoreal rendering <FeatureToggleTag /> {#photoreal}

<FeatureToggle />

A third render mode: a **path-traced**, photo-like picture of the design, with real light, shadows and reflections. Use it to show a customer the finished room, or for a still image. For everyday editing, Standard and High quality are quicker.

| You need | |
|---|---|
| **Photoreal on** for your organisation | Ask your BMR contact |
| **A browser with WebGPU** | Recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. See [WebGPU support](../../getting-started/webgpu#browsers) |
| **The 3D view** | Not the floor plan |

1. In **View** mode, pick **Photoreal** in the toolbar, or in the bar while [presenting](#presenting-a-design).
2. Keep the camera still. The picture refines (*Rendering 12 / 64*), then denoises.
3. Passes and denoising: **View settings** › **Lighting & environment**.

Photoreal missing, won't start or looks grainy? See [WebGPU support › Fix common problems](../../getting-started/webgpu#fix-common-problems).

## <Icon name="equipment" size="1em" /> Equipment {#equipment}

Place and arrange equipment, furniture, outlets, logos and murals. The **Room Configuration** tab shows your organisation's setup for this room, see [Default Room Configuration](default-room-configuration).

### Equipment tabs {#equipment-tabs}

| Tab | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Generic** | Placeholder items — a display, a chair — when the model doesn't matter yet. |
| **Catalog** | Your organisation's [Equipment Catalog](../../catalog/). |
| **Bundles** <FeatureToggleTag /> | Saved sets of equipment, placed in one go. |
| **Branding** | Your logos and [panel murals](#panel-murals). |

Equipment **snaps onto mounts** (VESA, rack, camera screw) and moves with them. Drag it off to detach.

### Bundles <FeatureToggleTag /> {#bundles}

<FeatureToggle />

A **bundle** is a reusable kit — for example a video bar, display, mount and table microphone.

1. Select **at least two** items in the room.
2. Choose **Create bundle**, name it, tick the **catalogs** and what it **Mounts to**.
3. **Save bundle**. Place it from the **Bundles** tab.

The placed copy is independent: editing the bundle later doesn't change rooms that already use it.

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-bundle-editor.jpg"
  alt="A bundle open in BMR Studio in its own small room — one wall with the floor in front of it — holding two displays, a video bar and a stand, with the Equipment sidebar showing the Generic, Catalog, Bundles and Branding tabs"
  caption="A bundle opens in its own one-wall room. Note the four Equipment tabs on the right."
/>

### Panel murals {#panel-murals}

A panel mural is your own picture printed across a group of acoustic panels. Create it under **Organisation › Preferences › Branding**, then place it from the **Branding** tab like any other item. It shows in the BOM as **Custom print**.

## <Icon name="cable" size="1em" /> Cables {#cable-mode}

Cabling starts from **what needs to be connected**, not from drawing cables.

Here you change cables, outlets and boxes. Other equipment stays put: click a device to see what's plugged in.

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-cable-connections-3d.jpg"
  alt="Cable mode in BMR Studio: the Connections panel on the left lists each device's ports with the connected port, cable type filters at the top and short warnings such as no free port, next to a see-through 3D room with the cables drawn in colour"
  caption="The Connections panel, next to the see-through 3D room."
/>

- **Connections panel** — each device lists its connections, **required** ones first. Pick a port, **Connect to another port**, or delete a cable. **Reset connections** starts over.
- **Required connections are drawn for you** when you open Cable mode. Ethernet runs through an outlet; **PoE** is supported.
- **Warnings sit on the device** — *Power missing*, *PoE missing*, *no free port* — with the fix one click away.
- **Filter by cable type** — Power, HDMI, Network, USB, Other — each with its own colour, also in **Plans**.
- **Cables save as you go.**

**2D, Split or 3D.** The 2D view is the **unfolded room**: walls, floor and ceiling laid out flat. Select a cable to reshape it on the grid; move sockets and equipment along guides (arrow keys nudge).

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-cable-split-view.jpg"

<FeatureToggle />

Recommends absorbers for the room and simulates the result against the **DIN 18041** target. You choose where treatment may go, then place and move the absorbers. Built on the room's [acoustic measurement](../../acoustics/acoustics-guide).

## <Icon name="copy" size="1em" /> Design versions

A room can have several **designs**, and each design keeps its own **versions**.

### Save and Save as New Design {#automatic-versioning}

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Save** | Saves your changes as a **new version** (*Version 2*, *Version 3* …). Earlier versions are kept. |
| **Save as New Design** (in the menu beside **Save**) | Makes a **separate design**, not linked to this one. |

- **Switching mode saves too**, as a new version, if you have unsaved changes.
- Cable changes **save automatically**.
- If a save fails, BMR Studio says why and offers **Retry**. Unsaved changes from an earlier session can be **restored** when you reopen the design.
- The **Versions** tab lists every version: **Activate** one to make it current, **Rename** or **Delete** the others.

## <Icon name="monitor" size="1em" /> Presenting a design {#presenting-a-design}

**Present** (Shift + F), next to the modes, takes the design full screen and steps through it one view at a time.

- **Standard views** first (Overview, Floor plan, …), then **your saved views**.
- **← / →** to step, jump to any view from the bar, switch to **High quality** or **[Photoreal](#photoreal)**, **take a screenshot**, **Esc** to stop.
- People you share a design with read-only can present it too.

### Saving your own views

In **View** mode, open the **Presentation** tab, set up the camera and click **Save current view**. Views can be renamed, reordered, replaced or removed.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Reviewing and activating a design {#-reviewing-and-approving-a-design}

The same for Studio designs and AR Designs:

1. Open the room's **Designs** tab.
2. Check the 3D layout, the cables and the **BOM**.
3. When the room is built to it, **Activate** the design — or **Set as active design** in BMR Studio, from the design's name menu in the toolbar.

The Active Design is the room's record. See [Deploy](../deploy/).

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> How to open BMR Studio

### On the web

1. Open the room at **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**.
2. Go to the **Designs** tab and pick a design — or create one with **New Design**. It opens in BMR Studio, full screen.

The room's Overview also links to **the active design in the Studio**.

### On iPhone or iPad

1. Open the room in BMR Mobile.
2. Go to **Designs** and open a design.

BMR Mobile opens existing designs. To start a new one in the app, use [AR Design](ar-design-guide).

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Tips

- **Can't select something?** It's in another mode. Tap **Switch**.
- **Review in View**: nothing moves by accident.
- **Start from a scan**: a good scan means less fixing in Structurals.
- **Every Save is a version**: go back from the **Versions** tab.
- **Save your views before a meeting**, then present with **Shift + F**.
