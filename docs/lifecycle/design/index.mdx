# <Icon name="design" size="1em" /> Design Phase

The **Design Phase** turns a room into a complete, validated setup. You can **create a design on the web** or **design in AR** on your phone — either way it lands in the room's Designs, ready to refine, version, and approve in **BMR Studio**.

## <Icon name="add-new" size="1em" /> Creating a design on the web

When you add a design on the web, the **Create Design** dialog asks for two choices:

**Design method**

- **BMR Studio** *(recommended)* — create and edit the design in BMR Studio's built-in 3D editor.
- **VRC (Workspace Designer)** — use Cisco's **Video Room Calculator / Workspace Designer** to create the room layout.

**Based on** — what the design starts from:

- **Scan** — an existing room scan, for an accurate model of the real space.
- **Existing Design** — start from a copy of another design for the room.
- **Custom Room** — a blank room you size yourself, when there is no scan yet.

Add a **Name** (required) and an optional **Description**, then **Save**.

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> BMR Studio

The unified 3D workspace for all room design work.

:::tip Design on a computer
BMR Studio is best used **on the web, on a computer** — the large screen and precise pointer make detailed design far easier. It also runs on **iPad**, though that isn't recommended for serious design work, and it is **not optimised for iPhone**.
:::

  src={useBaseUrl('/img/design/bmr-studio-ui.png')}

BMR Studio uses dedicated **modes** — View, Structurals, Equipment, and Cables — so you can focus on one thing at a time. Place equipment from the catalog, adjust room structure, plan cable routing, create multiple design versions, and mark the final design as **Active**.

**[Read the full BMR Studio guide &rarr;](bmr-studio)**

## <Icon name="3d-ar-design" size="1em" /> AR Design

Place equipment directly into the physical room using augmented reality on your iPhone or iPad. Items are positioned at real scale in the actual space, and the result is saved as a design that you can continue refining in BMR Studio.

AR Design requires a **room scan first** — the scan provides the coordinate space that links AR-placed items to the room model. Once uploaded, the AR Design is **imported into BMR Studio**, appearing alongside your Studio designs so you can refine, version, and finish it there.

**[Read the full AR Design guide &rarr;](ar-design-guide)**

## <Icon name="catalog" size="1em" /> Catalog

Both design tools draw from the **Equipment Catalog** — the shared library of items you place in a room, each with a 3D model and rich metadata. The catalog now has its own section covering what it stores and the 3D model standards behind it.

**[Go to Catalog & 3D Models &rarr;](../../catalog/)**

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Outputs of the Design Phase

- An **approved design** with a complete equipment list
- **Coverage visuals** — camera view, screen visibility, audio coverage
- **Acoustic recommendation** based on measurements and use case
- A **full version history** tied to the room

---

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Prerequisites

- ✅ Completed **scan** and **markers** — Room Data ready
- ✅ **Acoustic measurement** uploaded (strongly recommended)

> The better the Room Data, the faster the Design — and the fewer revisions needed.
