## <Icon name="add-new" size="1em" /> Creating a design on the web

Room › **Designs** › **New Design**.

| Field | Choose |
|---|---|
| **Design Method** | **BMR Studio** *(recommended)* |
| **Based on** | **Scan** — the real room · **Existing Design** — a copy · **Custom Room** — a box you size yourself |
| **Name** | Required. A description is optional. |

**Save** — the design opens straight in BMR Studio. A Custom Room opens in **Structurals**, to shape the walls.

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> BMR Studio

The 3D editor for all design work, with one **mode** per job: **View**, **Structurals**, **Equipment** and **Cables**.

:::tip Design on a computer
Use a computer. iPad works for review; iPhone is preview only.
:::

<Figure
  src="/img/design/bmr-studio-ui.png"

  caption="BMR Studio."
/>

**[Read the BMR Studio guide &rarr;](bmr-studio)**

## <Icon name="3d-ar-design" size="1em" /> AR Design

Place equipment in the real room, at real scale, on iPhone or iPad. Needs a **room scan first**. After upload, the AR Design sits with the room's other designs and opens in BMR Studio.

**[Read the AR Design guide &rarr;](ar-design-guide)**

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Outputs of Design {#-outputs-of-the-design-phase}
