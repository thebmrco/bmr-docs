# <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Share a Design

<PageIntro illustration="invite" meta={['Web platform', 'Admins']}>

Show a design to someone outside BMR. **One link. No account. View only.**

</PageIntro>

Anyone with the link sees **this design and nothing else**: not the room, not the location, not the rest of your organisation. They cannot change a thing.

---

## <Icon name="key" size="1em" /> Who can share {#who}

Only an organisation **Admin**. Location Managers and Room Managers don't see the button, whatever their rights in the room.

Why so strict? A link leaves your organisation. Hand it out on purpose.

---

## <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Create a link {#create}

1. Open the design in [BMR Studio](./bmr-studio).
2. Press **Share**, top right in the header.
3. Press **Create share link**.
4. **Copy link** and send it.

:::warning Copy it now
The link is shown **once**. BMR keeps only a fingerprint of it, so a lost link can't be brought back. Lost it? Remove access and create a new one.
:::

You can make as many links as you need, one for each person if you like. Each is listed in the same window.

---

## <Icon name="eye" size="1em" /> What the person sees {#recipient}

They open the link in a browser. No sign-in. The design opens with a **View only** badge, and the header shows where it is and what it's called.

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The whole room at a glance. Look from any side with the compass |
| **In Room** | Walk through it. Pick the eye height: **Walking, adult**, **On a chair** or **Wheelchair** |
| **Camera View** | Look through a camera of the design. **Previous camera**, **Next camera**, pan and tilt |
| **Coverage** | The marked area: what the cameras see, where the microphones pick up voices, where the speakers are heard well and the display can be read. Switch between **Camera**, **Microphone**, **Speaker** and **Display** |

Also in reach: the equipment list (**BOM**), the properties of what they click, a **Screenshot**, **Full screen**, and the BOM as **PDF** or **Excel**.

What they don't get:

- **Editing.** Nothing can be moved, added or saved.
- **Cables.** They are not shown in a shared design.
- **The people.** Who made the design is left out.

:::note Not every design can be shared
A [VRC design](../../integrations/cisco-vrc) <FeatureToggleTag /> can't be opened through a link yet. The person sees *This design can't be shared*.
:::

## <Icon name="activity-log" size="1em" /> Keep an eye on it {#manage}

Press **Share** again to see the links that still work:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **Expires** *date* | A link works for **30 days** |
| **Not opened yet**, **Opened once**, **Opened 4 times** | How often the person came. *last on* gives the day |
| **Extend 30 days** | Another 30 days from today. The link stays the same |
| **Remove access** | The link stops working **at once**. Confirm with **Remove access?** |

Above the list, **Opened 7 times through its links, old ones included** adds up the views of every link the design ever had, the ended ones too.

Someone opens an old link? They see *This link is no longer valid*. Ask the sender for a new one.

---

## <Icon name="organization" size="1em" /> Every link of the organisation {#organisation}

Admins also get one list for the whole organisation: **Organisation › Shared designs**.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Design** | The design, with its room and location. Click it to open it |
| **Shared by** | Who made the link, and when |
| **Views** | How many times it was opened, and the last day |
| **Expires** | The last day, or **Expired** or **Access removed** |

Search by design, room or location. **Show expired and removed links** brings the old ones back into the list. **Remove access** works from here too.
