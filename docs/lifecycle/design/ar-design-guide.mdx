# <Icon name="3d-ar-design" size="1em" /> AR Design Guide

<PageIntro illustration="ar-design" meta={['BMR Mobile', 'Beta']}>

Place equipment at real scale in the room, with AR on iPhone or iPad. Upload it as a design and continue in BMR Studio.

</PageIntro>

<Notice kind={['design', 'preview']} />

:::caution Upload before you leave
An AR Design is kept **only while the session is open**. Nothing is saved on the device until you upload. **Design 1** is created for you; rename it with the pencil.
:::

---

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Before you start

| The room has… | What happens |
|---|---|
| **A scan** | The app recognises the room, then you design. Full upload. |
| **No scan** | **Scan Recommended**: tap **Do Scan First**, or **Continue** in *limited mode*. |

**Limited mode:** only your photos can be uploaded. They go to the room's markers, without coordinates. Placed items are **not** uploaded.

---

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Recognising the room {#-room-verification}

The app matches the camera view to the room's [AR World Map](../../technology/ar-world-map), so items land in the scan's coordinates.

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Recognising this room** with a **% of room seen** ring | Turn slowly round the room. |
| **Keep turning left** / **Keep turning right** | Turn that way. |
| **Room recognised** | Wait: the design session opens by itself. |
| **Still can't recognise this room** (after about 20 s) | **Try again**, **Continue without positioning**, **Re-scan the room** or **Learn why this happens**. |

:::warning Skip positioning = limited mode
**Skip positioning** or **Continue without positioning** puts the design in limited mode. The banner says **Room not positioned, only images can be uploaded**: take photos with the shutter, as placed items are not uploaded.
:::

The same screen appears when you add **markers** or start an **acoustic measurement** in a scanned room.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Finishing up

- **One design**: **Upload & Exit**.
- **Several**: the **All designs** tab, then **Review & upload all**.

<StepFlow steps={[
  { label: "All designs lists every design; the open one is marked Current. Tap Review & upload all.", image: "/img/ar-design/Multiple-designs.jpg", alt: "All designs tab listing Design 1 and Design 2, with the current one marked Current and a Review & upload all button" },
  { label: "Design Preview: remove a design with the trash icon, then Upload all, or Back to designing.", image: "/img/ar-design/Review-multiple-design.jpg", alt: "Design Preview review step listing each design and its items, with Upload all and Back to designing buttons" },
]} />

**✕** asks **Leave AR Design?**:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| **Save & Exit** | Opens the review, then uploads. Shown only when there is something to upload. |
| **Exit** | Leaves and **discards** every design in the session. |
| **Go Back** | Back to designing. |

Uploaded designs appear in the room's **Designs**. Open one in **BMR Studio** to refine, compare it with other versions, and make it the **Active Design** once installed.

---

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Tips

- **Scan first, design second**: without a scan only photos upload.
- **Good light, slow turns**: the room is recognised faster.
- **One item at a time**: place each piece before adding the next.
- **Bring screens to life** with the wand.
- For cabling, coverage and detail, continue in **BMR Studio**.
