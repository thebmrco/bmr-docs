# <Icon name="3d-ar-design" size="1em" /> AR Design Guide

Place equipment directly into the physical room using augmented reality on your iPhone or iPad. Items are positioned at real scale in the actual space, and the result is saved as a design — ready to continue refining in BMR Studio.

:::info Designs save themselves
You never name or manually save an AR Design. One is created automatically — **Design 1** (rename any time with the pencil) — and kept **saved locally** as you work. It is **uploaded when you tap Upload & Exit** at the end of the session. Close the session with the **✕** button before uploading to discard it instead.
:::

---

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Before you start

A **room scan is required** before starting an AR Design. The scan provides the coordinate space that links AR-placed items to the room model. If no scan exists, the app prompts you to scan the room first.

---

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Room verification

When you start an AR Design in a scanned room, BMR Mobile first **relocalizes against the original scan** — so whatever you place is anchored to the correct spatial position. Move the device slowly around the space so it can match what it sees to the room scan.

You will see:

- **Verifying room…** while it looks for a match.
- **Room verified** once relocalization succeeds — you can then place equipment with confidence that it is anchored correctly.
- If it cannot find the room, you can **Retry** or **Continue without verification**.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Finishing up

When you are done, upload your work from the design drawer.

- **One design** — tap **Upload & Exit** and it uploads directly.
- **Several designs** — the drawer's **All designs** tab lists them all (the one you are editing is marked **Current**), and the button becomes **Review & upload all**.

Closing the session with the **✕** button before uploading discards your work.

Once uploaded, the AR Design appears in the room's **Designs** section alongside any designs created in BMR Studio. From there you can:

- Open it in **BMR Studio** to add more equipment, adjust placement, or refine the layout
- Compare it with other design versions for the same room
- Move it through approval and mark it as the **Active Design** when installed

## <Icon name="star" size="1em" /> Tips

- **Scan first, design second** — without a scan, AR-placed items cannot be anchored to the room model.
- **Good lighting** helps the app match the room faster during verification.
- **Walk around slowly** during verification so the app can recognise surfaces from the original scan.
- **Place items one at a time** — position each piece before adding the next for the most accurate result.
- **Bring screens to life** — use the wand to set screen content and preview how the room looks in use.
- AR Design is best for **initial placement and visualisation**. For detailed configuration, cabling, and coverage analysis, continue in **BMR Studio**.
