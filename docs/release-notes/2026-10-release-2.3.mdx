# Release 2.3 2026 October

Version 2.3 brings **BMR Mobile 2.3** (released 22 October) and the **Web Platform**'s October update. In the app, there is a **new way to add the room photo**, acoustic results are **easier to read**, and you can **share a link to a room** from the app. On the web, **BMR Studio** can now **present a design** full screen, step by step, with your own saved views. A new **render mode control** adds **High quality** rendering. Scans, markers, measurements and designs show **what they are linked to**, and the **catalog picker** is easier to search.

## Web Platform (2026.09.3 and 2026.10.0)

BMR Studio has a new **Present** button at the end of the mode switcher at the bottom (shortcut **Shift + F**). It takes the design full screen and steps through it one view at a time — ready to walk a customer or a colleague through the room.

- The presentation shows the **standard views** first (Overview, Floor plan, …), then **your saved views**, in order.
- To save a view, open the **Presentation** tab in **View** mode, position the camera and click **Save current view**. Saved views can be renamed, reordered, replaced with the current camera position or removed.
- While presenting, step with **← / →** (or Page Up / Page Down), **jump to any view** from the presentation bar, **take a screenshot**, and press **Esc** to stop.
- People you share a design with read-only can present it too.

- **Standard** — plain rendering, the quickest to work with.
- **High quality** — adds bounce lighting and ambient occlusion, for a more realistic look without waiting for a render.

Every scan, marker, acoustic measurement and design now shows **what it belongs to**, so you can see at a glance how a room's data fits together.

- In the room's data, each item shows a **Linked to** or **Built on** label — for example *Scan 12 Sep*, *via* a scan, *Custom room* or *Not linked*.
- An item that is not linked can be connected with **Link to a scan**.
- The same information appears on the **Designs**, **Scans** and **Acoustic Measurements** pages.

A set of smaller improvements makes working in BMR Studio feel more natural.

- **Walls and the ceiling push back** — an item you drag stops at a wall or the ceiling, then gives way if you keep pushing.
- **A hint in View mode** — try to drag equipment in View mode and BMR Studio tells you how to switch to Equipment mode.
- **The View menu opens in View mode** and stays collapsed in the editing modes, out of your way.
- **2D plan view** — switching to 2D centres the room and fits it to the canvas, and equipment and furniture are drawn with clear outlines.
- **Added walls** reach the ceiling and snap to the existing walls.
- **Arrow keys nudge** doors, windows and openings along their wall.

## BMR Mobile (2.3)

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">

    <Camera size={18} /> A new way to add the room photo

A room without a photo now shows a **camera icon** on its picture. Tap it to open **Hero pic** and choose:

- **Take photo** — photograph the room now.
- **Pick from markers** — use one of the room's marker photos, so you don't need a new picture.
- **Pick from library** — use a photo from your device.

The room photo is also a step in the **Next step** banner, after the scan, the markers and the acoustics: **Add a room photo · tap the camera**. When all four are done, the banner says **Room captured**, with **Continue on the web** to carry on in BMR Studio.

- **Measured results** now show **Reverberation**, **Bass balance** (Boomy, Balanced or Tight) and **Speech to noise** on colour scale bars. Tap a bar to see what it measures and which end is better.
- **Scores match the Web Platform.** The score bands and the combined result of the three measurement positions now come from BMR's servers, so the app and the web show the same score.
- **Compare 2 or 3 measurements.** In the room's **Acoustics** tab, tick the measurements you want, then tap **Compare**. The bar shows how many are selected.
- **An animated room** guides each measurement: it shows the speaker and how to move between the measurement spots. The speaker placement is also easier to read on iPad.
- The room confirmation screen shows the **room name** and the **scan date** separately, so a room named after a date is not mistaken for the scan.

Send a room from BMR Mobile to a computer or anyone on your team, and open it there straight away.

- When a room is captured, the banner says **Room captured** — tap **Continue on the web**.
- Or use **Share Room** in the room's **⋯** menu, or in the menu of any room in the room list.
- The standard iPhone and iPad share sheet opens: send the link with **Messages**, **Mail**, **AirDrop** or any other app, or **Copy** it.
- The link opens the room on the Web Platform in any browser — ready to continue in **BMR Studio** on a computer. On an iPhone or iPad with BMR Mobile 2.3, it opens the room in the app.

Tap **+ Create** to start anything from one place.

- **New room** — create the room first, then scan, mark, measure and design it.
- **In a room** — go straight to **Scan room**, **Add markers** or **Acoustic measurement**, then choose the room.
- **Structure** — **New location** and **New organisation**. Options your role can't use are shown greyed out, so the menu looks the same for everyone.

Creating needs an internet connection; without one, the options are greyed out.
