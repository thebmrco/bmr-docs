# Release 2.3 2026 October

Version 2.3 brings the **Web Platform** its October update. **BMR Studio** can now **present a design** full screen, step by step, with your own saved views. A new **render mode control** adds **High quality** rendering. Scans, markers, measurements and designs show **what they are linked to**, and the **catalog picker** is easier to search.

---

## Web Platform (2026.09.3 and 2026.10.0)

BMR Studio has a new **Present** button at the end of the mode switcher at the bottom (shortcut **Shift + F**). It takes the design full screen and steps through it one view at a time — ready to walk a customer or a colleague through the room.

- The presentation shows the **standard views** first (Overview, Floor plan, …), then **your saved views**, in order.
- To save a view, open the **Presentation** tab in **View** mode, position the camera and click **Save current view**. Saved views can be renamed, reordered, replaced with the current camera position or removed.
- While presenting, step with **← / →** (or Page Up / Page Down), **jump to any view** from the presentation bar, **take a screenshot**, and press **Esc** to stop.
- People you share a design with read-only can present it too.

In **View** mode, a new **render mode control** in the toolbar lets you choose how the room is drawn:

- **Standard** — plain rendering, the quickest to work with.
- **High quality** — adds bounce lighting and ambient occlusion, for a more realistic look without waiting for a render.

The 3D viewer now runs on **WebGPU** in browsers that support it, and falls back automatically in those that do not.

Every scan, marker, acoustic measurement and design now shows **what it belongs to**, so you can see at a glance how a room's data fits together.

- In the room's data, each item shows a **Linked to** or **Built on** label — for example *Scan 12 Sep*, *via* a scan, *Custom room* or *Not linked*.
- An item that is not linked can be connected with **Link to a scan**.
- The same information appears on the **Designs**, **Scans** and **Acoustic Measurements** pages.

Choosing a catalog in BMR Studio is quicker.

- **One searchable list** replaces the separate Favourites, Recent and Browse tabs.
- **Starred favourites come first**, and each catalog shows its owner and number of items.
- The catalog in use is marked **Current**. Click a catalog to select it.

A set of smaller improvements makes working in BMR Studio feel more natural.

- **Walls and the ceiling push back** — an item you drag stops at a wall or the ceiling, then gives way if you keep pushing.
- **A hint in View mode** — try to drag equipment in View mode and BMR Studio tells you how to switch to Equipment mode.
- **The View menu opens in View mode** and stays collapsed in the editing modes, out of your way.
- **2D plan view** — switching to 2D centres the room and fits it to the canvas, and equipment and furniture are drawn with clear outlines.
- **Added walls** reach the ceiling and snap to the existing walls.
- **Arrow keys nudge** doors, windows and openings along their wall.
