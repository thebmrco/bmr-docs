# November 2026: web 2026.11.x, BMR Mobile 2.4

This release is about working in **BMR Studio**. It opens in **View mode**, with a new header and one **Space Configuration** panel, and you choose how a design starts. **Replace**, **Repeat**, **Distribute** and **Align** make editing faster, **cables** follow the room, and **Room modes** show where a room booms. Every organisation gets a **catalog of its own**, and **Data Analysis** gives exact numbers per organisation, location and room. In **BMR Mobile 2.4**, **Continue scan** replaces Retake.

The web update is 2026.11.x, live on 24 November 2026. The matching app release is [BMR Mobile 2.4](#bmr-mobile-24). The release before this one is [Release 2.4 of October 2026](2026-10-release-2.4).

Features marked <FeatureToggleTag /> are switched on per organisation.

- **Studio opens in View mode.** Pick an item and **Edit** and **Delete** appear above it. **Edit** opens the mode that edits it, with the item still selected and the camera where it was. **Delete** removes it at once, with **Undo**. **Del** does the same. See [Editing](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#editing).
- **The modes read as the steps of a design:** View, Structurals, Equipment, Cables and Acoustics. A green arrow marks the current one and slides along when the mode changes.
- **A new header.** The design file control (thumbnail, breadcrumb and file menu), the view switcher (**2D**, **Split**, **3D**) with undo and redo, and at the far right who else is in the design, **Help** and **Share**. The mode alert in View mode and the configuration pill are gone.
- **A camera toolbar** above the modes: the preset views (**Overview**, **Top** and four **Sides**; Dollhouse is now **Overview**), **Focus** and zoom. Going to a preset or to a device's camera flies the view there, and **In room** and **Camera view** have a way back to 3D. See [Moving the camera](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#camera).
- **Center camera on object** and **F** show the object whole, head-on, with space around it.
- **Camera view** lists one entry per camera device and looks through its widest lens. Zoom goes as far as the lens's optical and digital zoom reach, and the view pans and tilts within the lens's range.
- **The classic compass** shows in every 3D view. The red half of the needle points north, and a click on **N**, **E**, **S** or **W** looks that way.
- **One Space Configuration panel** holds everything the room shows. The **Room** tab is gone. Each design also has its own **Background colour**, under **Lighting & environment**.
- **Coverage is in the header,** one type at a time: **None**, **Display**, **Microphone**, **Speaker** (when it is switched on for your organisation) or **Camera**.
- **Guidelines is now Room Configuration.**
- **On a phone,** every panel starts collapsed and only one opens at a time, so the room comes first.
- **Shared designs** open in a read-only Studio with the same controls: presets, **In room**, device cameras, the BOM and Properties. Cables are not shown in a shared design for now.

- **Welcome to BMR Studio.** The first time you open a design from a scan, you choose how it starts: **Placeholder blocks** (chairs blue, tables green, other objects purple), **Generic equipment** or **Remove everything**. Or pick a **room configuration**, with the ones recommended for the room's size first. A Custom Room gets the same choice: the empty room, or a room configuration. The Studio tour waits until you have chosen, and no tour starts over a dialog. See [the welcome](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#the-welcome).
- **Automatic design from scans** is an organisation setting, off by default. An Admin switches it on in **Preferences › General**. Then a processed scan builds the room's design and makes it the **Active design**: each object the scan found becomes a generic 3D model of its type, at its size and place and in its colour. Walls, floor, doors, windows and openings come from the scan. Things that don't belong in a meeting room, such as a bed, a bath, kitchen appliances, stairs or a fireplace, are left out. A new scan makes a new baseline: it becomes the Active design, and the previous one stays as a version.
- **Scan models.** Under **Preferences**, choose which catalog item a scanned chair, table, cupboard, sofa or display becomes. See [Scan models](../structure/organisation#scan-models).
- **A scanned display keeps the size the scan found.** It stands on a cart when it stood on the floor, and the BOM counts it as a generic display.
- **A design you create from a scan** starts with the default equipment, like the generated one.
- **Room configurations place themselves.** Add one and BMR Studio puts it where it fits: against the best-fitting wall, or centred on the floor when nothing is wall-mounted. Other walls where it fits are offered, and what doesn't fit is marked **Does not fit**. If items are in the way, Studio offers to remove them first. A room configuration keeps its wall colours and materials when you reopen the design.

- **Replace** shows the whole catalog with the items of the same type first. Hover a card and the item appears in place of the selection, so you see it before you swap. See [Replace](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#replace).
- **Repeat, Select all of type, Distribute and Align.** **Repeat** works for every item: wall items repeat along or up their wall, table items at their own height. **Select all of type** picks every item like the one you clicked, also a wall the camera cuts away, shown see-through. **Distribute** spaces items out between two walls you click, and **Align** lines them up in a row or a column. See [Repeat, Distribute and Align](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#arrange).
- **Structurals: Box and Cylinder,** with guides, wall snapping, Scale, Repeat, Distribute and Align.
- **Rotate and Orient are one tool.** In the Rotate panel, **Point to** a wall or screen, and selected chairs turn to face it, or **Rotate individually**.
- **Every floor item snaps flush to a wall,** and the grid snaps items by their sides.
- **New equipment lands in view:** inside the room or the bundle area, on its own surface, clear of other items and outlined. Items face you while you place them, and a wall item finds a free spot on the wall. What stands between the camera and the selection turns see-through.
- **Placement warnings only for real clashes** — not for a laptop on a table. An item outside the room says so, and placing holds at the walls.
- **Live wall lengths,** with end dots and arrows to drag a wall end along its own line. A wall that nearly lines up with another no longer stretches towards infinity when the other wall moves.
- **Fixed-size chairs, round and oval tables,** and table resizing.
- **Hidden objects** can't be picked, in any mode.
- **The Properties tab reads as name and value,** in every mode. With nothing selected it shows the room and the project: size, floor area, seats, equipment, cables, who created the design and when. With an item selected it shows its head, its details and a **Layout** section for dimensions, position and rotation. See [Properties](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#properties).
- **The BOM lists every item in the room,** catalog or not, in a panel on the left. It follows every add, replace, remove, undo and redo at once, without saving first. See [BOM](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#bom).

Cable mode is still **Beta**. Check a route before you build from it.

- **Cables follow the room.** Routing runs on a graph of the room's edges and corners, and it is the new default. Cables follow angled walls instead of zigzagging on the grid, and run along the ceiling, walls or floor depending on where their ends are.
- **Shape a route.** Select a cable and a green pill sits on each run you can slide, in 3D and in the unfolded 2D view. A cable to a device on a table has a drop-point handle, and every segment has a handle you can grab. **Route via** sets whether the cable meets on the floor or the ceiling, and **Reset route** goes back to the automatic one. A run you slide by hand is kept for this session only. See [Shaping a route](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#cable-route).
- **Five groups of cable types,** each with one icon and one colour, everywhere, and one cable type dropdown.
- **Hover and select on cables,** with **Re-route** and **Delete**. The panel and the room share the same hover and selection, and cables get default labels.
- **A tidier Connections panel.** The header has hide, add equipment, **Unselect all** and **Reset to default**. Every connection row looks the same: **Select** or **Connection missing**. A removed connection frees its far port for other devices, and a clicked component is selected on its own, not with its group.
- **Cable properties** show type and length at the top, your own label and only free ports, with **Undo** after a delete.

- **Room modes, before and after.** In the **Diagnostics** tab of Acoustics mode, one switch shows the room before and after treatment. - **Loudspeaker coverage rates every seat.** Directivity per frequency band, with a **1 kHz / 4 kHz** switch. A seat off a speaker's axis gets quieter instead of being cut off, and the rings are measured from the loudest place in the room. It is the **Speaker** choice in the **Coverage** menu. - **Measurement charts.** The ambient noise chart draws the hearing threshold. The hearing threshold, RT60 and noise come first, **Show average** draws only the average, noise is averaged as energy, and tab names follow your language.

- **Treatment rows** pick their product from a searchable list.

## BMR Mobile (2.4) {#bmr-mobile-24}

BMR Mobile 2.4 follows [BMR Mobile 2.3](2026-10-release-2.3#bmr-mobile-23).

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <Icon name="scan" size="1.1em" /> Scan
  </span>
</summary>

- **Continue scan replaces Retake.** When a scan ends, one bar offers **Continue scan** and a checkmark to save. **Continue scan** reopens the camera in the same room: your scan, markers and photos stay, everything new merges in and markers stay on their walls. The **i** button explains the three uses: add markers, extend the room, fix a gap. <FeatureToggleTag />
- **A live model while you scan.** A small model of the room builds as you scan and carries over when you continue. <FeatureToggleTag />
- **A scan that stops by itself says why** and what to do next: the device lost its position, is too hot, ran low on memory or reached the size limit. <FeatureToggleTag />
- **Tips for markers** are a plain list of icons: power outlets, network points, displays and AV, tables, chairs and lamps.
- **One dialog says why a scan is missing.** Before an acoustic measurement, new markers or an AR Design, the app names the problem: **Scan Needed** (no scan yet), **Re-scan Needed** (the scan has no AR Map), **Scan Not Uploaded Yet** (it is still waiting to upload) or **AR Map Unavailable** (it could not be loaded). It says what happens if you carry on without it: an invalid MOS score, markers saved as photos only, or designs that can't be saved. It offers to scan or re-scan right away, to try again, or to **Continue Anyway**.
- **Your organisation decides whether the mesh is captured.** The app reads the setting before each scan. On: the 3D mesh is captured and uploaded with the scan. Off: it is not. Change it on the Web Platform and it applies to the next scan, with no app update. <FeatureToggleTag />
