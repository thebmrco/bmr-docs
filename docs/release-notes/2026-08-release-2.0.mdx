# Release 2.0 2026 August

The iOS app has been rebuilt and adapted to Apple's **Liquid Glass** design language, and the **MOS score** now includes the room's **background noise**.

:::info The App Store app is now named "BMR (Legacy)"
This release ships to the existing iOS app, which has been **renamed to BMR (Legacy)** in the App Store. It is the same app you already use, still available through your existing **download link** — it is not searchable in the App Store.

Over the coming weeks we will publish a brand-new, **publicly searchable app called BMR Mobile**. Once it is available it becomes the app to install going forward, and these docs will point you to it. Until then, keep using **BMR (Legacy)**.
:::

## Web Platform

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <Cable size={18} /> Cabling infrastructure & auto-generated cabling documentation (BMR Studio) (BETA)

Plan the full cabling infrastructure of a room in **BMR Studio** and generate professional cabling documentation from it — built for installers and AV/IT teams.

:::info BETA
Cabling and Cabling Docs are in beta, but available to all users.
:::

**Placement in Studio (Cables mode)**

- Place **floorboxes, ceiling boxes, and conduits** alongside existing devices and outlets.
- Boxes snap to their correct surface; outlets can be mounted in boxes and conduits terminate at boxes/outlets.
- Every outlet, box, and conduit gets an **auto-numbered label** (e.g. `D-04`, `P-02`, `FB-1`, `CB-3`), with per-port labels for multi-port outlets and support for manual overrides.

Power outlets can now also be placed on **floors and tables** — not just walls — so floor boxes and table-mounted outlets are represented accurately and act as connection points in the cabling workflow.

<img src="/bmr-docs/img/release/2026-08-cabling-studio.jpg" alt="BMR Studio Cables mode with floorboxes, ceiling boxes, and conduits placed and auto-numbered labels applied" style={{width: '100%', maxWidth: '900px', display: 'block', margin: '1rem auto', borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #E5E5D5'}} />

**Cabling Docs**

A new **Cabling Docs** area generates documentation directly from the saved design:

- **Placement diagrams** — floor plan, wall elevations, and reflected ceiling plan, annotated with labels.
- **Cable diagram** — a schematic view of what plugs into what, grouped by conduit with fill counts.
- **Patching plan** — a printable patch schedule, pre-created for every ethernet outlet-port.

All diagrams reuse the labels entered once in Studio, can be viewed in the app, and exported as **PDF**. Built-in consistency checks warn about unlabelled outlets, duplicate labels, missing patch targets, and over-filled conduits.

## Acoustics

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <AudioLines size={18} /> Background noise now included in the MOS score
  </span>
</summary>

The room's **background noise** is now factored into the **MOS score** as a penalty, so scores more accurately reflect real-world speech intelligibility.

- Background noise is applied as a **Speech-to-Noise Ratio penalty** in the MOS calculation — a noisier room lowers the score according to the acoustic model.
- Implemented entirely in the **back-end**: the iOS app and web platform automatically receive the updated MOS values, with existing measurements remaining fully compatible.

</details>

---

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <Filter size={18} /> More resilient background-noise measurement
  </span>
</summary>

We've improved how the room's **background noise** is calculated during an acoustic measurement, making it more **resilient to transient sounds**.

- The measurement now **filters out short peaks** in the background-noise window — a clap, a cough, or a brief comment during the quiet measurement period is removed rather than inflating the result.
- The result better reflects the room's true, **steady-state noise floor**.
- This is about removing **peaks** only. A **consistent** noise lasting the whole measurement — for example someone talking loudly right next to the microphone throughout, or persistent HVAC — cannot be filtered out and will still affect the result.

The graph below illustrates the effect: **BEFORE**, brief peaks pulled the measured background noise upward; **NOW**, those peaks are filtered out and the value tracks the actual noise floor.

<img src="/bmr-docs/img/release/2026-08-background-noise-peak-filtering.svg" alt="A-weighted background-noise level over time. A wavy black curve with several transient peaks. A dashed grey BEFORE line sits high because the peaks inflate it, while a solid green NOW line sits low, tracking the room's true noise floor once the peaks are filtered out." style={{width: '100%', maxWidth: '800px', display: 'block', margin: '1rem auto', borderRadius: '8px', border: '1px solid #E5E5D5'}} />

</details>

Because the MOS calculation evolves as we add more acoustic factors, the MOS score circle now shows **which variant** a measurement was made with:

- **RT** — Reverberation Time
- **RT-BR** — Reverberation Time + Bass Ratio
- **RT-BR-N** — Reverberation Time + Bass Ratio + Background Noise *(new in this release)*

The variant label is displayed **under the score ring**, replacing the "out of 5" indication, and is shown consistently in both the **Library list** and the **acoustic measurement view**.
