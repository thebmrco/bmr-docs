# Release 1.4 2026 July

This release brings a major upgrade to **BMR Studio** — automatic design versioning, improved structural editing, and smarter cable routing — alongside a redesigned **Room Overview**, a new **acoustics experience** on the Web, and an improved acoustic measurement flow in BMR Mobile.

It also includes everything previously shipped in the **1.4 BETA** release.

---

## Web Platform

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <LayoutGrid size={18} /> Equipment & Catalog — Place Default Room Configuration items from Guidelines
  </span>
</summary>

Recommended equipment from your **Default Room Configuration** can now be placed straight into the room from the **Guidelines** panel — no need to switch to the Catalog. The panel shows which guideline categories are fulfilled and which items are still missing, with a **Place in room** action for anything below guidelines.

Placement itself is smoother too:

- Equipment **stays selected during rotation**, so you can fine-tune without reselecting.
- Newly placed devices are **auto-selected** and show **Advanced properties** by default.

Your work is never overwritten:

- **Versions are automatically created when saving or changing mode.** Previous versions can be reviewed, renamed, activated, or deleted at any time from the **Versions** tab.

- **Designs are now shown as a list** in the room design view, making it easier to identify and open the right design.

- **Outlets are now shown in the Cable Guide overview**, giving a complete picture of the room's cabling and connection points.

- New **Tidy Bundles** option for cleaning up cable routing.
- **Auto-Route** and **Tidy Bundles** are labeled **BETA** to indicate their current working state.

The acoustic measurement flow now anchors every measurement to the right room and the right position:

- At the start, a **Confirm Room** pop-up with the room's scan thumbnail lets you verify which room you are in before starting the measurement. If the thumbnail does not match where you actually are, **Exit** and open the right room first.
- During the process, **updated room detection** accurately pinpoints the location of the **speaker** and the **measurement points** within the room — powering the new measurement positions view on the Web.

  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <Scan size={18} /> See when BMR Mobile recognises your room
  </span>
</summary>

When you start a new **AR Design**, add a new **Marker**, or run a new **Acoustic Measurement** in a scanned room, BMR Mobile now relocalizes against the original scan first — so whatever you capture is anchored to the correct spatial position in the room. You see **Verifying room…** while it looks, then **Room verified** once relocalization succeeds. If BMR Mobile cannot find the room, you can **Retry** or **Continue without verification**.
