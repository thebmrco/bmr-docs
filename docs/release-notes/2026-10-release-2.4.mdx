# Release 2.4 2026 October

Version 2.4 brings the **Web Platform** its second October update (released 22 October). **Cable mode** is rebuilt around a **Connections** panel and a flat **unfolded-room** view. **Bundles** let you save a group of equipment and place it again in one go. Measured rooms get an **acoustic optimisation proposal**, and each room gets a **Documents** tab with its **inventory** and **bill of materials**.

The matching app release is [BMR Mobile 2.3](2026-10-release-2.3#bmr-mobile-23).

Features marked <FeatureToggleTag /> are switched on per organisation.

Cabling in BMR Studio now starts from **what needs to be connected**, not from drawing cables.

- **Connections panel.** Every device lists its connections, the **required** ones first and the rest under **More**. Choose a port from the list — **Use another port** or **Connect to another port** — or remove a cable. **Reset connections** starts over.
- **Required connections are drawn for you** when you open Cable mode. Ethernet always runs through an outlet, and **PoE** is supported.
- **Warnings sit on the device** they are about — for example *Power missing*, *PoE missing* or *no free port* — with the fix one click away.
- **Filter by cable type** — Power, HDMI, Network, USB and Other — each with its own colour, the same in 3D, 2D and **Plans** (formerly Cabling Docs).
- **Unfolded room.** Switch between **2D**, **Split** and **3D**. The 2D view lays the walls, floor and ceiling out flat: select a cable and reshape it on the grid, move sockets and equipment along guides (arrow keys nudge), and place sockets on any surface.
- **See-through 3D.** In Cable mode, furniture is hidden and walls are faded so the cables stand out.
- **Cables save as you go**, and rerouting runs in the background, about six times faster.
- Side panels **minimise to a rail** to give the room more space. Designs you can't edit open Cable mode **read-only**.

A **bundle** is a reusable kit — say a *Huddle room kit* with a video bar, display, mount and table microphone. Save it once, then place the whole set in any room.

**Create a bundle**

1. In BMR Studio, select **at least two** items in the room.
2. Choose **Create bundle**, give it a **Bundle name** and an optional description.
3. Tick the **catalogs** it should be available in, choose what it **Mounts to** — Floor, Wall or Ceiling — and click **Save bundle**.

**Place a bundle**

- In **Equipment** mode, open the **Bundles** tab (next to Generic, Catalog and Branding), search, and drag the bundle into the room.
- Each placed item shows **Part of "…"**. The placed copy is independent — editing the bundle later does not change rooms that already use it.

**Manage bundles**

- **Organisation › Preferences › Bundles** lists every bundle your organisation can place: its own, public ones and ones shared with it. Your own can be edited there.
- A bundle opens in its own small room — **one wall, the floor in front of it and the ceiling above** — so you can arrange the kit before you use it.
- A bundle can be **shared** with other organisations, which can place it but not change it.
- **Room Configurations** can include bundles, so a recommended setup can bring a whole kit.

See what acoustic treatment from your catalog would do for a room, before you design it.

- A measurement page now has two tabs: **Measured results** and **Optimisation proposal**.
- Click **Optimise this room** and enter **how many people use this room**. BMR simulates the room, matches materials from your catalog and scores the treated room.
- The proposal shows the **acoustic score after treatment**, the **average RT60** before and after, whether the room lands **inside the DIN 18041 tolerance band**, and the **recommended materials**. Estimated values are marked **Estimated · not measured**.
- **Create new acoustic design in BMR Studio** turns the proposal into a design you can refine.
- A room **without a measurement** gets a **Simulated** acoustics page, estimated from assumed materials — a starting point until the room is measured with the app.
- The room overview shows the score **with this design** and **vs potential**.
- **Organisation › Preferences › Acoustic** has a **Default acoustics catalog** for proposals.

**Build the inventory from your markers**

The inventory starts from the marker photos taken in the room. Click **Review N markers** to go through them one by one. After a new scan, **Carry over the items of the previous inventory** to start from what was there before.

**The inventory**

- Every item shows its **category**, **manufacturer**, **SKU**, **quantity**, a note, whether it is **verified**, and the **marker it came from**.
- When every marker is reviewed, the inventory is marked **Inventory verified**. Each change is kept as a **version**.
- **Add** an item by hand — choose it from the catalog or type it in. A photo is optional: it makes a marker, and the item counts as verified.
- Switch between a **list** and a **photo grid**, and **download** the inventory.

**The bill of materials (BOM)**

- The **BOM** lists what a design needs — equipment with category, manufacturer, SKU and quantity, and the **cables** by type and length.

- **Add a room configuration** — **Start from scratch** or **Start from an existing one**.
- A configuration has **placed** equipment, arranged in BMR Studio, and an **unplaced** list.

**Acoustic panels** improve how a room sounds — and now they can show **your own picture** too. A **panel mural** spreads one image across a group of acoustic panels, so the treatment that fixes the room's acoustics also carries your brand, a city skyline or any artwork you choose.

**Create a mural** in **Organisation › Preferences › Branding › Panel murals** with **New mural**:

- **Upload the picture** — drag and drop a PNG or JPEG, up to 150 MB.
- **Choose the acoustic panel** — a product from your catalog that allows branding, or a **custom size** — and the grid, from **1 × 1** up to **3 × 3**.
- **Set the panels** — width, height, depth, the **gap** between panels and the **bleed** for printing.
- **Decide how the picture meets the gaps** — let it **run behind the gaps**, or **split it at the panel edges** to keep the whole picture. You can also **wrap the sides** with a mirror of each panel's edge.
- **Place the picture** — **Fill wall** or **Fit inside**, with a background colour. The dialog shows the **wall size**, the **print size per panel** and the **print density**.

**Use it**

- In BMR Studio, the mural is in the **Branding** tab in Equipment mode — place it on the wall like any other item.
- It appears in the bill of materials as **Custom print**.
- **Download print files** for the panels, at the print density you choose (for example 150 dpi).
