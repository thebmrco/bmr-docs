# Release 2.2 2026 September

Version 2.2 of **BMR Mobile** makes the app easier to use: a new **room recognition flow**, the choice between an **Advanced** and a **Basic** acoustic measurement, and **in-app guidance** that shows you what to do — and in which sequence.

---

## BMR Mobile (iPhone / iPad)

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">

Room recognition detects which room you are in by matching the camera view against the room's scan. It is what anchors your work to the 3D room:

- **AR Design** — merges what you do in AR with the scan.
- **Markers** — positions markers added outside a scan.
- **Acoustic measurements** — positions the speaker and each measurement.

The redesigned flow gives live feedback while it runs: a **"% of room seen"** indicator, directional hints (*"Keep turning left"*), and a clear **"Room recognised"** confirmation.

The acoustic measurement now runs in one of two modes. The difference is **position detection** — the acoustic result is identical.

- **Advanced (default)** — detects the position of the **speaker** (with a photo) and of **each measurement**, placing them in the 3D room. Requires a room scan and an internet connection.
- **Basic** — skips position detection entirely. The measurement starts faster and works offline.

The app picks Advanced automatically whenever possible and falls back to Basic otherwise.

:::note Set in the app only
The mode can currently only be changed **in the app**, under **Settings → Acoustic Settings → Advanced Measurement Mode** — not from the web platform. The default is **Advanced**.
:::

The app now shows you **what to do and in which sequence**:

- The room overview always shows the **next step** — from *"Start here — Scan the room"* to *"Finish design in BMR Studio on web"*.
- Completed steps are **ticked off**, and each card explains its step in one line.
- A one-time guided tour introduces the sequence: **Scan → Acoustics → AR Design**.
