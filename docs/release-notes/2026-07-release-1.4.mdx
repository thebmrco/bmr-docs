# Release 1.4 (BETA) 2026 July

This is a **BETA** release focused on BMR Mobile — clearer feedback when the room is being recognised, full **offline** acoustic measurements, and a series of smaller refinements across acoustics, notes, and notifications. No Web platform changes in this release.

---

## BMR Mobile (iPhone / iPad)

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <Scan size={18} /> See when BMR Mobile recognises your room
  </span>

When you start a new **AR Design**, add a new **Marker**, or run a new **Acoustic Measurement** in a scanned room, BMR Mobile now relocalizes against the original scan first — so whatever you capture is anchored to the correct spatial position in the room. You see **Verifying room…** while it looks, then **Room verified** once relocalization succeeds. If BMR Mobile cannot find the room, you can **Retry** or **Continue without verification**.

No connection? Full acoustic measurements — including the **three-position guided flow** — now work without the network. Add a short note describing where you took the measurement, and it queues up in the **Notifications** tab. When you are back online, upload each measurement individually and assign it to the right room.

Before starting a new acoustic measurement, BMR Mobile now shows a **Confirm Room** screen with the room's scan thumbnail and name. Confirming here ensures the measurement is tied to the correct room and its scan — if the thumbnail does not match where you actually are, **Exit** and open the right room first.

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">
  <span style={{display:"inline-flex", alignItems:"center", gap:"10px"}}>
    <LineChart size={18} /> Cleaner T20 (RT60) chart
  </span>
</summary>

The **T20 (RT60) graph** now only plots reverberation times the backend considers **valid**, so unreliable bands do not distract from the useful data. Fewer misleading spikes; the shape of the curve is easier to read at a glance. Bands that were excluded are shaded and labelled **Invalid measurement**.

You can now add and edit **notes** on a **group** of related acoustic measurements — not just on individual ones. Useful when a bundled three-position measurement shares a single story that belongs at the group level rather than repeated on each position.
