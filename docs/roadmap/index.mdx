## Next (3–6 Months)

<RoadmapItem icon={Gauge} title="BMR Room Evaluation (Beta)">
  First version of a room evaluation engine that uses metrics to score how the technology is placed in a room and the experience it will deliver. Beta release with basic functionality.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={AudioLines} title="Advanced Acoustic Engine">
  Improved noise floor handling and higher measurement consistency.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Palette} title="BMR Studio Mode Refinement">
  BMR Studio modes (View, Structurals, Equipment, Cables (Beta), and Acoustics as a feature toggle) are already available in the current version. Going forward, each mode will be refined significantly — clearer guidance, sharper tools, and tighter workflows per mode.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Palette} title="BMR Studio Refinement">
  Improved Active Design validation, version handling, and design-to-deployment workflows.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={RefreshCw} title="Webex Two-Way Synchronization">
  Webex Control Hub sync already imports locations and workspaces into BMR. The next step is two-way sync, so changes made in BMR flow back to Control Hub.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={AudioLines} title="Room Auralizations">
  Listen to simulated room acoustics to validate design decisions before deployment.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={ScanSearch} title="Improved Object Detection">
  Enhanced recognition accuracy and reduced manual corrections during capture.
</RoadmapItem>

## 6–12 Months

<RoadmapItem icon={Gauge} title="BMR Room Evaluation — Released">
  Released version of the room evaluation engine, expanding on the Beta with a broader metric set, refined scoring, and full integration into the design and deployment workflows.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={RefreshCw} title="Lifecycle Automation">
  Smart workflow transitions and automated progression across the room lifecycle.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={RefreshCw} title="Extended Automation Across the Full Room Lifecycle">
  Deeper automation from Discovery through Design and into Maintenance operations.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={RefreshCw} title="Maintenance Expansion">
  Expanded monitoring, room health insights, and maintenance workflows at scale.
</RoadmapItem>

<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">Delivered — already available</summary>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="BMR Studio">
  Web-based Studio environment for design management with dedicated modes (View, Structurals, Equipment, Cables (Beta), and Acoustics as a feature toggle). Design on the web; review in BMR Mobile on iPhone and iPad.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="Active Design & Lifecycle Flow">
  All designs versioned in BMR Studio with a clear Active Design as the authoritative room record.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Copy} title="Automatic Design Versioning">
  Versions created automatically on save or mode change. Review, rename, activate, or delete from the Versions tab, plus Save as New Design.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="AR Design">
  Real-time 3D placement with structured outputs linked to the Active Design. Create designs directly from AR.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="Acoustic Workflow">
  Guided multi-position acoustic measurement with room confirmation, spatially anchored speaker and measurement positions, ambient noise chart with acceptability zones, Xi20/Xi30 metrics, and a full offline flow.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={ScanSearch} title="Room Detection & Relocalization">
  Relocalizes against the original scan when adding a marker, AR design, or acoustic measurement — anchoring captures to the correct spatial position and pinpointing speaker and measurement points.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="Self-Serve Onboarding">
  Users who sign up without an invitation are guided through creating their first Organisation, Location, and Room, on both Web and BMR Mobile.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Compass} title="Compass">
  Room orientation captured during scan and visualised in BMR Studio.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="Notes">
  Persistent notes on all elements — organisations, locations, rooms, scans, markers, acoustics, and designs. Available on Web and App.
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={CircleCheck} title="Room Configurations">
  Admins define recommended equipment setups for a room size range, next to BMR Defaults. **Place configuration** drops a whole layout into a design. [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4)
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Presentation} title="Presentation Mode and High Quality Rendering">
  Present a design full screen, step by step, with your own saved views. Standard and High quality render modes. [Release 2.3](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.3)
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Cable} title="Cable Mode with Connections">
  Cabling starts from a Connections panel: required connections drawn for you, warnings on the device, and an unfolded-room 2D view. Still Beta. [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4)
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={FileText} title="Documents: Inventory and BOM">
  Every room has an inventory built from its markers, and a bill of materials for each design. [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4)
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Image} title="Acoustic Panel Murals">
  Your own picture printed across a group of acoustic panels, with print files. [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4)
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Package} title="Equipment Bundles (feature toggle)">
  Save a set of equipment and place it again in one go. Switched on per organisation. [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4)
</RoadmapItem>

<RoadmapItem icon={Wand2} title="Acoustic Optimisation Proposal (feature toggle)">
  See what acoustic treatment from your catalog would do for a measured room, then open it as a design. Switched on per organisation. [Release 2.4](../release-notes/2026-10-release-2.4)
</RoadmapItem>
