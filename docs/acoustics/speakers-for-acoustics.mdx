<details className="bmr-details">
<summary className="bmr-summary">About these figures</summary>

- All figures come from the manufacturers' datasheets; room sizes assume a <U m={2.8} /> ceiling. A quieter room allows a
  larger room, a noisier one a smaller one.
- **<U m2={175} />** is where the check stops: <U m3={500} /> at a <U m={2.8} /> ceiling, the largest volume DIN 18041 covers for
  video-conference rooms. 35 dB(A) is the background-noise limit DIN 18041 recommends for meeting rooms.
- **JBL Xtreme 4** — no maximum level published; its room size is an estimate from the 70 W battery rating.
- **Cisco Room Bar / Room Bar Pro** — level published without a measuring distance; 1 m assumed (±3 dB).
- **Typical JBL portable** — class average of the Clip 5, Flip 6 and Charge 5; the widest uncertainty of the set.
- **JBL Clip 5** — its <U mm={45} /> driver may also lose the 125 Hz band.

</details>

## <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Supported connections

- **Bluetooth** (preferred)
- **AirPlay** — needs WLAN access!
- **USB-C** or **HDMI** (wired) — the cable limits where you can stand while measuring

Pick the speaker as the **Output device** on Step 1 of the acoustic flow.

## What to look for (quick)

- **Powered speaker**, clean output **80 Hz–15 kHz+**
- Loud enough for the room — the maximum sound level tells you, the wattage alone does not: check it in the [Loudspeaker Check](./loudspeaker-check/)
- Stable placement on a **stand at ~<U m={1.3} />** (seated head height)
