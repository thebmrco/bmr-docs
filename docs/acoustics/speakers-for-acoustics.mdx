# <Icon name="volume-2" size="1em" /> Speakers for acoustics

Below are loudspeakers suitable for BMR's Acoustic Measurements. The room sizes come from the
[Loudspeaker Check](./loudspeaker-check/) for a room with 35 dB(A) background noise, the limit DIN 18041 recommends for
meeting rooms, and a 2.8 m ceiling. They are the largest rooms in which the measurement should give the full result,
including the bass bands the MOS score needs. A quieter room allows a larger one, a noisier room a smaller one.

175 m² is where the check stops: 500 m³ at a 2.8 m ceiling, the largest volume for which DIN 18041 states its target
for inclusive communication rooms, the usage type that lists the video-conference room. All sizes are estimates from
the datasheets. JBL publishes no maximum level for the Xtreme 4, so its level is estimated from its power.

The Jabra Speak2 75 suits smaller meeting rooms. Use it in music mode: speak mode is rated from 150 Hz, so the 125 Hz
band is likely to drop out, and the MOS score is then calculated without its Bass Ratio.

:::tip Not on this list?
Enter its datasheet values in the [Loudspeaker Check](./loudspeaker-check/).
:::

## <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Supported connections

BMR Mobile can drive the measurement speaker over any of:

- **Bluetooth** (preferred)
- **AirPlay** — needs WLAN access!
- **USB-C** (wired) — cable reduces freedom to choose measurement location in room
- **HDMI** (via adapter) — cable reduces freedom to choose measurement location in room

Whichever you use, pick the speaker as the **Output device** on Step 1 of the acoustic flow.

## What to look for (quick)

- **Powered speaker** (no external amp), clean output **80 Hz–15 kHz+**
- Loud enough for the room: the wattage alone does not tell you, the maximum sound level does. Check it in the [Loudspeaker Check](./loudspeaker-check/)
- Stable placement on a **stand at ~1.3 m** (seated head height)

*This page only lists speaker choices. A separate guide will cover how to run the acoustic measurement.*
