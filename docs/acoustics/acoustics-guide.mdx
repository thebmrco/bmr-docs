# <Icon name="audio-lines" size="1em" /> BMR Mobile Acoustics Tutorial

<PageIntro illustration="acoustics" meta={['BMR Mobile', 'An external speaker']}>

Measure your meeting room's acoustics with BMR Mobile on iPhone or iPad. You get a **Mean Opinion Score (MOS)** and the **reverberation time (RT60)** — the starting point for any acoustic improvement.

</PageIntro>

<Notice kind="acoustics" />

BMR Mobile guides you step by step. You measure at **three spots you choose** in the room, and BMR combines them into one result.

---

## <Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> Advanced vs Basic

The measurement runs in one of two modes. **Advanced is the default** — BMR Mobile uses it whenever it can, and falls back to Basic otherwise.

| | Advanced (default) | Basic |
|---|---|---|
| **Speaker photo** | Required in room setup | Not captured |
| **Room recognition step** | Yes — places everything in the 3D room | Skipped |
| **Measurement spots** | Recorded in the 3D room, visible on the web | Not recorded |
| **Requirements** | Room scan + internet connection | None |
| **Acoustic result (MOS, RT60)** | Identical | Identical |

The mode comes from the **organisation default** on the web (**Organization settings → Preferences → Acoustic**), unless a device overrides it in **Settings → Acoustic Settings → Advanced Measurement Mode** — see [Organisation](../structure/organisation).

| Situation | What BMR Mobile does |
|---|---|
| Room has no scan | Asks you to scan first (**Scan Recommended**). **Continue** runs Basic, with no MOS score: it needs the room volume from a scan |
| You are offline | Runs Basic and shows a hint |
| Advanced turned off in Settings | Runs Basic and shows a hint on how to turn it on |

:::tip Why we recommend Advanced
The position and speaker data captured in Advanced mode will be used in upcoming releases to further improve measurement quality and to enable **Auralisation** — letting you *listen* to how a room actually sounds.
:::

### Reading the result {#reading-the-result}

The green end of each bar is better. Tap a bar to see what it measures.

| On screen | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Mean Opinion Score** | How easy it is to talk in the room — 5 is best. **Good** starts at **3.6**. See [Understanding the MOS Score](mos-score) |
| **Variant** (e.g. **RT+BR+N**) | What the score includes: Reverberation + Bass balance + background Noise |
| **Reverberation** | Room acoustics: how long sound lingers |
| **Bass balance** | Room acoustics: **Boomy**, **Balanced** or **Tight** |
| **Speech to noise** | With ambient noise: how far speech rises above the room's noise |
| **Average RT60** | Average reverberation time across the spots |
| **All Measurements** | Each spot and the average, or just the average |

A good meeting room scores **3.6 or more**, with an RT60 inside the DIN 18041 target for its size — see [Reading acoustic results](reading-acoustic-results).

### Optimisation proposal <FeatureToggleTag /> {#optimisation-proposal}

<FeatureToggle />

1. Room page, under the score: **Optimise**. Or in the results: **Optimisation proposal → Estimate**.
2. Enter **how many people use this room** → **Save & optimise**.
3. BMR simulates the room, picks materials from your catalog and shows the **predicted score**. Change the catalog to recalculate.

The same proposal is on the web — see [Reading acoustic results](reading-acoustic-results#optimisation-proposal).

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Impulse Method (Balloon Pop)

For when no speaker is available. A balloon pop or a hand clap triggers the measurement.

:::caution
Less reliable than the speaker sweep. Hand claps are inconsistent — use them only as a last resort.
:::

1. Turn on **Settings → Acoustic Settings → Enable Impulse Mode**.
2. Start a measurement and choose **Impulse** (Balloon pop or hand clap) under **Measurement mode**.
3. Wait until the room is quiet, then **pop the balloon** (about 30 cm from the device) or clap once.
4. Stay silent for about 3 seconds, then review and save.

Impulse results also show **ambient noise**, like the sweep.

## <Icon name="offline-mode" size="1em" /> Measuring Offline

<a id="measuring-offline"></a>

No connection? The whole measurement works **offline**, as the **Basic** flow.

1. Measure as usual.
2. Add a short **note** saying where you are, so you can find it later.
3. Back online, BMR Mobile **uploads it automatically**.
4. A measurement not linked to a room waits in **Notifications** — tap **Assign Room**.
