# <Icon name="audio-lines" size="1em" /> BMR Mobile Acoustics Tutorial

Capture accurate acoustic data from your meeting room using BMR Mobile on iPhone or iPad. The measurement gives you **reverberation time (RT60)** and a **Mean Opinion Score (MOS)** — the foundation for any acoustic improvement recommendation.

BMR Mobile guides you through a step-by-step flow, taking measurements at three positions in the room for a reliable, representative result.

---

## <Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> Advanced vs Basic

The measurement runs in one of two modes. **Advanced is the default** — the app picks it automatically whenever it is possible, and falls back to Basic otherwise.

| | Advanced (default) | Basic |
|---|---|---|
| **Speaker photo & position** | Captured during room setup | Not captured |
| **Room recognition step** | Yes — the room is recognised so everything is placed in the 3D room | Skipped |
| **Measurement positions** | Recorded in the 3D room, visible on the Web | Not recorded |
| **Requirements** | Room scan + internet connection | None |
| **Acoustic result (MOS, RT60)** | Identical | Identical |

The mode comes from the **organisation default** set on the web under **Organization settings → Preferences → Acoustic**, unless a device overrides it in the app under **Settings → Acoustic Settings → Advanced Measurement Mode** — see [Organisation settings](../organisation/settings). If the room has no scan yet, or you are offline, the app automatically runs the Basic flow and shows a hint explaining how to get Advanced back.

:::tip Why we recommend Advanced
The position and speaker data captured in Advanced mode will be used in upcoming releases to further improve measurement quality and to enable **Auralisation** — letting you *listen* to how a room actually sounds.
:::

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Before You Start

**You will need:**
- iPhone or iPad with BMR Mobile installed
- A speaker connected over **Bluetooth**, **AirPlay**, **USB-C**, or **HDMI** — see [Recommended Speakers](speakers-for-acoustics)
- A quiet room

**Prepare the room:**
- Close all windows and doors
- Turn off HVAC, fans, and any noise sources
- Keep the device's microphone uncovered
- Place the speaker **at least 1 m / 3 ft away from the wall**

The results screen shows:

| Result | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Mean Opinion Score (MOS)** | Predicted speech quality — 5 = excellent, 1 = poor |
| **MOS variant** | Which factors the score includes (e.g. **RT+BR+N** = Reverberation Time + Bass Ratio + Background Noise) — see [Understanding the MOS Score](mos-score) |
| **Average RT60** | Average reverberation time across all positions taken |
| **RT60 graph** | T20 reverberation time across frequency bands |
| **Expert Mode** | Off shows the essential metrics only; on reveals the full set of graphs and parameters |

A good meeting room typically targets **RT60 < 0.5 s** and **MOS ≥ 3.5**.

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Impulse Method (Balloon Pop)

Use this method when a speaker is not available. A balloon pop or hand clap triggers the measurement.

:::caution
Hand claps are inconsistent — use only as a last resort. The balloon method is less reliable than the sweep method.
:::

1. Tap the **Balloon icon** to start. Wait until the ambient noise level is low.
2. **Pop the balloon** (held ~30 cm from the device) or clap sharply once when prompted.
3. Remain silent for ~3 seconds while the app captures the decay.
4. Review and save results.

Balloon and impulse measurements now surface **ambient noise** alongside the impulse result — the same context you get with the sweep flow — so you can interpret the number the same way regardless of method.

## <Icon name="offline-mode" size="1em" /> Measuring Offline

No connection? The full acoustic measurement — including the three-position guided flow — works **without the network**. Offline measurements run as the **Basic flow**, since the Advanced flow requires an internet connection.

- Run the measurement exactly as you would online.
- Add a short **note** describing where you took the measurement, so you can identify it later.
- The measurement queues up in the **Notifications** tab.
- When you are back online, upload each measurement individually and assign it to the right room.
