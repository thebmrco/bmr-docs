# <Icon name="acoustics" size="1em" /> Reading Acoustic Results on the Web

Once an [acoustic measurement](acoustics-guide) is uploaded, you review it on the web platform at **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**. The acoustics page is designed to put the most important information first and make it easy to compare measurements.

For how the underlying numbers are calculated, see [Understanding the MOS Score](mos-score).

---

## <Icon name="target" size="1em" /> What the page shows

The acoustics page leads with the two figures that matter most, then supports them with detail:

- **Overall acoustic score** and **average RT60** at the top — the headline read on how the room performs.
- A **compact measurement list** — every measurement taken in the room, so you can open, compare, and manage them.
- A **Room scan & measurement positions** view *(beta)* — see below.

The **MOS score is always shown on the Room Overview** too, using the linked measurement when one is set and otherwise the latest measurement. See [The Room Page](../getting-started/room-page).

---

## <Icon name="marker" size="1em" /> Room scan & measurement positions *(beta)*

The page includes a **Room scan & measurement positions** view that shows exactly where each part of the measurement happened, laid over the room's scan:

- The **speaker** position.
- Each **measurement point** (A, B, C) where a reading was taken.

Because BMR Mobile now pinpoints these locations during the [measurement flow](acoustics-guide), you can see the spatial spread of a measurement — how far apart the points were, and where they sat relative to the speaker and the room — rather than reading numbers in isolation.

## <Icon name="audio-lines" size="1em" /> Supporting charts

Each measurement also surfaces the detail charts described in [Understanding the MOS Score](mos-score):

- The **T20 (RT60) chart**, plotting only the frequency bands the platform considers valid, with invalid bands shaded and labelled.
- The **ambient noise chart**, with Ideal / Acceptable / Too loud acceptability zones.
