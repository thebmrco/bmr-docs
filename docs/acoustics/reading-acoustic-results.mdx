# <Icon name="acoustics" size="1em" /> Reading Acoustic Results on the Web

<PageIntro illustration="analyse" meta={['Web platform']}>

Once an [acoustic measurement](acoustics-guide) is uploaded, review it on **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)** — the most important figures first, then the detail.

</PageIntro>

<Notice kind="acoustics" />

For what the score means, see [Understanding the MOS Score](mos-score).

---

## <Icon name="target" size="1em" /> What the page shows

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-07-acoustics-positions.png"
  alt="Acoustics page with overall acoustic score, average RT60, measurement list, and a room plan showing the speaker and measurement positions"
  caption="Score and RT60 first, then the measurements and where they were taken."
/>

| Part | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Overall acoustic score** | The MOS of the measurement. For a group of spots, the **average** of the spots |
| **Average RT60** and **Ambient Noise** | Reverberation time and background noise, averaged |
| **Acoustic Score incl. Ambient Noise** *(Beta)* | Shown beside the overall score when the background noise was measured |
| **Measurement list** | Every measurement in the room — open, compare or manage them |
| **Room scan & measurement positions** | Where the speaker and each spot were — see below |

**Compare** up to **4** at a time. A whole group counts as one, its average.

The **Room Overview** shows the score of the room's **newest measurement that has a score**. See [Room](../structure/room).

## <Icon name="marker" size="1em" /> Room scan & measurement positions {#-room-scan--measurement-positions-beta}

The room's scan with the **speaker** and each **measurement spot** on it — so you see how far apart the spots were, and where they sat relative to the speaker.

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **2D** | Top-down floor plan |
| **3D scan** | The 3D room scan, including furniture |
| **3D mesh** | The raw scanned mesh |

Positions are recorded only in the **Advanced** flow of the [measurement](acoustics-guide#-advanced-vs-basic). Older measurements and Basic measurements have none.

## <Icon name="audio-lines" size="1em" /> Supporting charts

| Chart | Read it like this |
|---|---|
| **T20 (RT60)** | The curve against the **DIN 18041 tolerance bands** and the **Target RT60** — inside is good, above is too reverberant |
| **Ambient noise** | Background noise against **Ideal / Acceptable / Too loud** zones |

More on both: [The Green Zone](mos-score#-the-green-zone) and [Ambient Noise Chart](mos-score#ambient-noise-chart).

## <Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> Score split by cause <FeatureToggleTag /> {#score-split-by-cause}

<FeatureToggle />

One ring splits the score into what holds it back:

| In the ring | Means |
|---|---|
| **Your score** | What the room delivers today — reverberation, bass and ambient noise all counted |
| **Room acoustics** | Points lost to reverberation and bass — absorbers win these back |
| **Ambient noise** | Points lost to background noise — only a quieter room wins these back |

Bars for **reverberation**, **bass** and **noise** show each lever. A warning line appears when the capture looks wrong — noise too low to be real, very high, or varying a lot between spots. Measure again before you trust that score.

## <Icon name="wrench" size="1em" /> Optimisation proposal <FeatureToggleTag /> {#optimisation-proposal}

<FeatureToggle />

See what acoustic treatment from your catalog would do for a room, before you design it.

1. Open a measurement — it has two tabs: **Measured results** and **Optimisation proposal**.
2. Click **Optimise this room** and enter **how many people use this room**.
3. BMR simulates the room, matches materials from your catalog and scores the treated room.
4. **Create new acoustic design in BMR Studio** turns the proposal into a design you can refine.

| The proposal shows | |
|---|---|
| **Acoustic score after treatment** | Estimated, from your score today |
| **Average RT60** | Before and after |
| **DIN 18041** | Whether the room lands inside the tolerance band |
| **Recommended materials** | From your catalog — change the catalog to recalculate |

Estimated values are marked **Estimated · not measured**.

- A room **without a measurement** gets a **Simulated** acoustics page, estimated from assumed materials — a starting point until the room is measured with the app.
- **Organisation › Preferences › Acoustic** has a **Default acoustics catalog** for proposals.
