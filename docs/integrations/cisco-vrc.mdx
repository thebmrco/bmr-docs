# <Icon name="puzzle" size="1em" /> Cisco Video Room Calculator Integration

<PageIntro illustration="integrations" meta={['Web platform']}>

Plan Cisco rooms with Cisco's **Video Room Calculator (VRC)** inside BMR, and open BMR designs in **Cisco Workspace Designer**.

</PageIntro>

<Notice kind="design" />

| Path | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **VRC design** <FeatureToggleTag /> | A design made in Cisco's Video Room Calculator, stored in BMR | Room › Designs › **New Design** › Design Method **VRC (Workspace Designer)** |
| **Export to Workspace Designer** | Any BMR Studio design you can edit, opened in Cisco Workspace Designer | BMR Studio › **Export** › **Workspace Designer** |

:::info Web only
VRC designs are created and edited on the web at **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**. BMR Mobile shows them with a web-only message.
:::

---

## <Icon name="layout" size="1em" /> What it does <FeatureToggleTag /> {#-what-it-does}

<FeatureToggle />

- Edit the room in Cisco's **2D layout**.
- **Add and configure Cisco devices** — endpoints, codecs, peripherals.
- Follow **Cisco's recommended room setups** and guidelines.

---

## <Icon name="palette" size="1em" /> Preview in Cisco Workspace Designer

In BMR Studio, **Export** (toolbar) → **Workspace Designer** copies the room and opens Workspace Designer. There, press **Ctrl + I** (**Cmd + I** on Mac) to import it.

- Cisco's own 3D preview.
- Cisco's planning and configuration tools.

BMR stays the record: versions, room data and the Active Design are kept in BMR.

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Lifecycle integration

A VRC design in BMR:

- Is one of the room's **designs**, on the Room page.
- Can be **activated** like any other design — see [Deploy](../lifecycle/deploy/).
- Sits alongside the room's other designs for comparison.
