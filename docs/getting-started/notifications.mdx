# <Icon name="notifications" size="1em" /> Notifications

<PageIntro illustration="updates" meta={['BMR Mobile', 'Web platform']}>

Everything that needs you, behind the **bell**. The number on it says how much.

</PageIntro>

| Where | Open it |
|---|---|
| **BMR Mobile** | Tap the **bell** in the top right corner of **Home** |
| **Web** | Click the **bell** in the header · **View all notifications** for the full list |

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> The bell badge reflects real state {#-the-bell-badge-reflects-real-state}

In BMR Mobile the number adds up:

| Counted | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Tasks** | Tasks assigned to you |
| **Unread notifications** | New messages, such as a task completed |
| **No-room archives** | Captures saved offline, not uploaded yet |
| **Failed uploads** | Uploads that need another try |

**0** means nothing is waiting. On the Web, the badge counts unread notifications.

---

## <Icon name="offline-mode" size="1em" /> Offline captures queue here {#-offline-captures-queue-here}

Work you did **offline** is **not uploaded automatically**, not even when you are back online. It waits here, with the note you added, until you upload it.

Back online:

1. Tap the **bell** in the top right corner of **Home**.
2. Tap an item under **No Room Archives** (**Assign Room**).
3. Pick the **Organisation**, **Location** and **Room** it belongs to.
4. Upload it. Repeat for **each item**, one at a time.

Something that **failed to upload** waits under **Failed Archives**. Tap it to try again.

## <Icon name="delete" size="1em" /> Clearing notifications {#clearing}

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| **BMR Mobile** | **Mark all as read** |
| **Web, bell** | Tick to mark one as read, or the bin to delete it · **Mark all as read** |
| **Web, View all notifications** | **Mark all as read** · **Delete all read** |
