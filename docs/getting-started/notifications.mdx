# <Icon name="notifications" size="1em" /> Notifications

The **Notifications** tab in BMR Mobile is where the app collects everything that needs your attention — and the **bell badge** tells you how much of it is outstanding.

---

## <Icon name="target" size="1em" /> The bell badge reflects real state

The number on the bell counts everything you actually need to act on:

- **Unread notifications**
- **Tasks** assigned to you
- **Pending no-room archives** — captures saved without a room yet assigned
- **Failed uploads**
- **Incomplete acoustic measurements**

When the badge shows **0**, there is nothing waiting for you — the count is a reliable signal rather than a decoration.

---

## <Icon name="offline-mode" size="1em" /> Offline captures queue here

Notifications is also where work you did **offline** waits to be finished:

- An [acoustic measurement taken offline](../acoustics/acoustics-guide#measuring-offline) queues in Notifications with the note you added describing where you took it.
- When you are back online, **upload each measurement individually** and **assign it to the right room**.
