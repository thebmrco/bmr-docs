# Account Setup

There are **exactly two ways** to start using BETTERMEETINGROOMS. Which one is yours comes down to a single question:

## Does your company already use BETTERMEETINGROOMS?

If anyone at your company already uses BETTERMEETINGROOMS, ask for an **invitation**. Starting your own creates a separate, empty workspace — you won't see your colleagues' rooms and data.
:::

## <Icon name="user-check" size="1em" /> Option 1 — Join your organisation (by invitation) {#by-invitation}

Your company already uses BMR — you don't create anything. An admin invites you into the **existing organisation** with a **role** that scopes what you can see and manage.

| Role | Scope | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Org Admin** | The whole organisation | Manage every location and room, and invite other users. |
| **Location Manager** | One location | Manage the rooms and data inside that location. |
| **Room Manager** | One room | Work on that single room — scans, markers, measurements, and designs. |

Invitations are sent by your organisation's admin from the **Users** tab — see [Inviting Users](inviting-users). If your company isn't set up in BMR yet, email **support@bettermeetingrooms.com**.

### <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Step 1 — Accept your invitation

Your invitation email contains a **link to download the app** and a **link to set your password**. Open the sign-up link and choose a password.

:::caution Use the invited email address
Sign up with the **exact email the invitation was sent to** — that's the address linked to your account, your organisation, and the rooms your colleagues see. A different address creates a separate account with no access to your team's data.
:::

### <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> Step 2 — Log in

Once your password is set, log in via **BMR Mobile** or the **Web** — both use the same Welcome screen.

**Via BMR Mobile** — install **[BMR Mobile](https://apps.apple.com/app/bmr-mobile/id6804977641)** from the Apple App Store and tap **Login**:

## <Icon name="add-new" size="1em" /> Option 2 — Start your own (from nothing) {#create-your-own}

Nobody at your company uses BMR yet — you build the workspace yourself, from nothing.

### <Icon name="user" size="1em" /> Step 1 — Sign up

Sign up either way — both lead to the same place:

- **Web** — go to **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**
- **App** — install **[BMR Mobile](https://apps.apple.com/app/bmr-mobile/id6804977641)** from the Apple App Store and open it

Both show the same **Welcome** screen. Tap **Sign up** (below the Continue button), then enter the **email address** and **password** you want to use on the next screen.

  src={useBaseUrl('/img/account-setup/Account-setup-sign-up.png')}

### <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Step 2 — Confirm your email

After signing up you'll receive a **confirmation email** at the address you registered with. Confirm it — you can't continue until you do.

:::caution No email?
Check your spam folder, and make sure you signed up with the address you meant to. The confirmation goes to exactly the address you registered.
:::

### <Icon name="add-new" size="1em" /> Step 3 — Sign in and create your workspace

Sign in after confirming. A welcome screen explains that you're not part of any organisation yet, with a **Get started** button that opens a short three-step wizard.
