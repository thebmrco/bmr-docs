## <Icon name="user-check" size="1em" /> Join your organisation {#by-invitation}

When your company already uses BMR, an admin invites you into the organisation with a **role** that scopes what you can see and manage.

| Role | Scope | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Org Admin** | The whole organisation | Manage every location and room, and invite other users. |
| **Location Manager** | One location | Manage the rooms and data inside that location. |
| **Room Manager** | One room | Work on that single room — scans, markers, measurements, and designs. |

Invitations are sent by your organisation's admin from the **Users** tab — see [Inviting Users](inviting-users). If your company isn't set up in BMR yet, email **support@bettermeetingrooms.com**.

### <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Step 1 — Accept your invitation

Your invitation email contains a **link to download the app** and a **link to set your password**. Open the sign-up link and choose a password.

:::caution Use the invited email address
Sign up with the **exact email the invitation was sent to** — that's the address linked to your account, your organisation, and the rooms your colleagues see. A different address creates a separate account with no access to your team's data.
:::

### <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> Step 2 — Log in

Once your password is set, log in via **BMR Mobile** or the **Web** — both use the same Welcome screen.

**Via BMR Mobile** — install **BMR Mobile** from the Apple App Store and tap **Login**:

## <Icon name="add-new" size="1em" /> Start your own {#create-your-own}

No invitation needed. Sign up directly — on the **Web** or in **BMR Mobile** — and BMR guides you through creating your own workspace instead of dropping you into an empty account.

### <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Step 1 — Confirm your email

Right after signing up you'll receive a **confirmation email** at the address you registered with. Confirm it — onboarding can't start until you do.

:::caution No email?
Check your spam folder, and make sure you signed up with the address you meant to. The confirmation goes to exactly the address you registered.
:::

### <Icon name="add-new" size="1em" /> Step 2 — Create your workspace

Sign in after confirming. A welcome screen explains that you're not part of any organisation yet, with a **Get started** button that opens a short three-step wizard.

:::tip Joining a company that already uses BMR?
Prefer an **invitation** (or sign up with your **company email**) rather than creating a brand-new organisation — otherwise you'll end up in a separate workspace and won't see your colleagues' rooms and data.
:::
