# Account Setup

There are two ways to get into BETTERMEETINGROOMS:

- **With an invitation** — someone at your organisation, or the BMR team, invites you. This is the right path if your company already uses BMR, because it places you in the correct organisation alongside your colleagues. Follow the invited steps below.
- **Without an invitation** — you sign up on your own and a short wizard helps you create your first Organisation, Location, and Room. Jump to [Signing up without an invitation](#signing-up-without-an-invitation).

---

## Invited by your organisation

### <Icon name="key" size="1em" /> Step 1: Request Access

Contact us at **support@bettermeetingrooms.com** to request access. Once approved, you will receive an **invitation email** containing:

- A **link to download the app** from the Apple App Store
- A **link to sign up** and set your password

---

### <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Step 2: Set Your Password

Open the sign-up link from the invitation email and create your password. **Use the same email address the invitation was sent to** — that is the address linked to your account, your organisation, and the rooms and data your colleagues see.

:::caution Important
If you sign up with a different address than the one the invitation was sent to, you will end up in a separate account and will not be able to access the same resources as your colleagues. Always use the exact email the invitation came to.
:::

---

### <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> Step 3: Logging In

Once your password is set, you can log in via **BMR Mobile** or the **Web**. Both use the same Welcome screen for authentication.

#### Via BMR Mobile

Download **BMR Mobile** from the **Apple App Store** using the link in your invitation email and tap **Login** to be taken to the Welcome screen.

#### Via the Web

Go to **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)** — you are taken directly to the Welcome screen. Enter your email and password to log in. The web platform gives you access to room management, acoustic comparisons, and **BMR Studio**.

## Signing up without an invitation

You do not have to wait for an invitation to get started. If you sign up **without** one — on the Web or in BMR Mobile — BMR guides you through creating your own workspace instead of dropping you into an empty account.

### 1. Confirm your email

Right after signing up, you will receive a **confirmation email** at the address you registered with. Open it and **confirm your email address** — the onboarding cannot start until this is done.

:::caution No email?
Check your spam folder, and make sure you signed up with the address you meant to. The confirmation is sent to exactly the address you registered.
:::

### 2. Create your workspace

Once your email is confirmed, sign in. A welcome screen explains that you are not yet part of any organisation, and a **Get started** button opens a short three-step wizard.

:::tip Joining a company that already uses BMR?
Prefer an **invitation** (or sign up with your **company email**) rather than creating a brand-new organisation — otherwise you will end up in a separate workspace and won't see your colleagues' rooms and data.
:::

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> After Logging In

You can now access the app or the web platform and start setting up rooms.

> If you were invited, make sure your company has already been set up in BMR. If not, contact your project lead or email **support@bettermeetingrooms.com**.
