# <Icon name="my-tasks" size="1em" /> My Tasks

<PageIntro illustration="organise" meta={['Web platform', 'BMR Mobile']}>

The work someone asked you to do, in one list. Open it, pick one, go.

</PageIntro>

| Where | Open it |
|---|---|
| **Web platform** | **My Tasks** in the menu, or **Open Tasks** on [Home](./web-platform#home) |
| **BMR Mobile** | The **My Tasks** tab |

---

## <Icon name="laptop" size="1em" /> On the web {#on-the-web}

**My Tasks** lists every task assigned to you.

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Room** | The room the task belongs to |
| **Task Name** | What to do, such as *Create Scan* |
| **Target** | What the task is about |
| **Assignment Date** | When it was given to you |
| **Completion Date** | When it was done. Empty while it is open |
| **Last Updated** | The last change |

Search by **Task Name** and filter by **Status**: **All Tasks**, **Pending** or **Completed**. Nothing for you? The list says *No tasks assigned to you.*

---

## <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> In BMR Mobile {#in-bmr-mobile}

The **My Tasks** tab shows your **Open tasks**. Nothing waiting says *No tasks*.

Tap a task and the app opens its room on the right tab, so you can start:

| Task | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Create Scan** | [Scan the room](../lifecycle/discovery/room-scan) |
| **Create Marker** | [Take the markers](../lifecycle/discovery/markers) |
| **Create Acoustic Measurement** | [Measure the room](../acoustics/acoustics-guide) |
| **Create AR Design** | [Place equipment in AR](../lifecycle/design/ar-design-guide) |

Every other task is done on the web. The app says so: *Web only action. Go to app.bettermeetingrooms.com for this task.*

---

## <Icon name="users" size="1em" /> Give a task to someone {#assign}

Two places, one idea: pick the person, they get the task in **My Tasks**.

| From | How |
|---|---|
| **A room** | On the [Room page](../structure/room#-the-room-page), the **Assign Task** panel lists the tasks that fit the room. Pick one, choose a person under **Select user...**, then **Assign** |
| **An inventory item** | On a [marker in the Inventory](../lifecycle/discovery/inventory#assign-a-marker-task), **Assign a marker task** |

The person list shows people who have access to the room. Nobody to pick? Give them access first, see [Inviting users](./inviting-users).

Once a task is assigned, the panel shows who has it. From there:

| To | Use |
|---|---|
| Hand it to someone else | **Reassign** |
| Take it back | **Cancel assignment** |
| Open a finished task again | **Reopen Task** |

Done tasks show the date and the person who did them.

---

## <Icon name="notifications" size="1em" /> When it is done {#when-done}

When a task is completed, the right person hears about it: a **Task Completed** [notification](./notifications).

- Someone else did the work: **the person it was assigned to** is told.
- The assigned person did it: the **Room Manager** is told. No Room Manager on the room? The **Location Manager**, then the **Admin**.
