# <Icon name="room" size="1em" /> The Room Page

Every room has a home page that brings its data together in one place — on the **Web** as the **Room Overview**, and in **BMR Mobile** as the **Room view**. It is where you check a room's status at a glance and jump into scans, markers, acoustics, and designs.

---

## <Icon name="layout" size="1em" /> Room Overview on the Web

The Room Overview page presents the room's key information up front:

- **Room details, ratings, and capabilities** at a glance.
- The **acoustic score (MOS)** — always shown, using the linked measurement when one is set and otherwise the latest measurement. See [Reading Acoustic Results](../acoustics/reading-acoustic-results).
- An **Active** section that previews the **active Design** together with the **scan, acoustic measurements, and markers** it is based on — so you can see, in one view, the design that represents the room and the data behind it.

## <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> Room view in BMR Mobile

The same room is available in **BMR Mobile**, where recent improvements make it easier to work with a room on-site:

- **Newly created rooms are shown at the top** of the room list, so a room you just made is easy to find.
- The **active Design** and the **linked scan, markers, and acoustic measurement** are clearly marked, so you can tell at a glance what the room is built from.
- **Designs can be deleted** directly from inside the Room view.
- A **web-only message** is shown for [VRC designs](../integrations/cisco-vrc), which are edited on the Web.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Moving a room

To move a room to a different organisation or location, open the Room Overview page, tap **Edit**, choose the new organisation and location, and save. See the [FAQ](faq) for a quick reference.
