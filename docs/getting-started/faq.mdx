# <Icon name="help-circle" size="1em" /> Frequently Asked Questions

---

### <Icon name="key" size="1em" /> Account & Access

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> How do I sign up?

**BMR Mobile** is available on the **Apple App Store**, and the web platform is at **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)**. There are two ways in:

- **With an invitation** — if a colleague or the BMR team has invited you, use the invitation link so you land in the correct organisation. Sign in with your company email and password, or via Google or Cisco Webex SSO. See [Account Setup](account-setup).
- **Without an invitation** — you can sign up on your own on the Web or in BMR Mobile. After signing up you first **confirm your email address** via the confirmation email you receive; then a short onboarding wizard guides you through creating your first **Organisation**, **Location**, and **Room**, so you land in a ready-to-use workspace instead of an empty account.

If you are joining a company that already uses BMR, prefer the invitation route (or use your company email) so you see the same rooms and data as your colleagues.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Can I use a personal email (like Gmail or Outlook)?

You can, but if you are part of a company you will not see the same rooms and organisation data as your colleagues. Use your company email to be placed in the correct organisation.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> I have an app called "BMR Mobile Private" — which one should I use?

Use **BMR Mobile**, now available publicly on the **Apple App Store**. The earlier **BMR Mobile Private** app (distributed by a download link) is **no longer supported** — delete it and install **BMR Mobile** from the App Store.

### <Icon name="smartphone" size="1em" /> Devices & Requirements

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Which devices support room scanning?

iPhones and iPads with **LiDAR** — iPhone 12 Pro or newer (Pro / Pro Max), iPad Pro 11" (2nd gen, 2020) or newer, iPad Pro 12.9" (4th gen, 2020) or newer. See [System Requirements](system-requirements) for the full list.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Can I access BMR on the web?

Yes. Go to **[app.bettermeetingrooms.com](https://app.bettermeetingrooms.com)** — the web platform is where you manage rooms, compare acoustic measurements, run organisation-wide search, and work in **BMR Studio**, the 3D environment for design and the Active Design record.

### <Icon name="lifecycle" size="1em" /> Lifecycle Phases

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> What is the Discovery Phase?

Discovery is where you capture the room with an iPhone — a scan, markers and photos, and a guided acoustic measurement. All of this becomes the room's **Room Data**, which every later phase builds on.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Should I use the Advanced or Basic acoustic measurement flow?

We recommend **Advanced**. It captures the **position of each measurement and of the speaker** (including a photo) during the flow — data we use to improve measurement quality, and which will enable **Auralisation** (hearing how a room sounds) in future releases. **Basic** runs the measurement without capturing those positions. Advanced needs a **room scan** and an **internet connection**; you choose the mode in **Settings → Acoustic Settings**.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> What is the Design Phase?

Design turns your Room Data into a validated room setup. There are **two ways to start**: create a design on the **web** — in **BMR Studio**, or via **VRC (Workspace Designer)** — or place equipment in **AR** on your iPhone or iPad. Both end up in **BMR Studio**, where you refine and version the design (best done **on a computer**). You can design the room yourself — placing equipment from the catalog, reviewing coverage overlays, and managing versions — or use [Send Feedback](send-feedback) to ask the BMR team for help. See [Design Phase](../lifecycle/design/).

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Is BMR Studio available to me?

Yes — BMR Studio is in **Beta** but enabled for everyone. It is designed for the **web on a computer**, which gives the best experience for detailed design; it also runs in BMR Mobile on **iPad** (good for reviewing, less suited to detailed work) and **iPhone** (preview only). Open any room, go to **Design**, and click **Open Studio** to get started.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> What is AR Design, and do I need to save it?

AR Design lets you place equipment directly in the physical room, at real scale, using your iPhone or iPad. You never name or manually save it — a design is created and **saved automatically** as you work, and **uploaded when you tap Upload & Exit**. It is then imported into **BMR Studio** to refine. A **room scan is required first**. See the [AR Design guide](../lifecycle/design/ar-design-guide).

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Can I add notes to rooms and other elements?

Yes. You can add notes to organisations, locations, rooms, scans, markers, acoustic measurements, and designs — on both the web platform and the app. You can also add a note to a **group** of related acoustic measurements (for example a bundled three-position measurement), not just to individual ones. Notes are visible to all team members with access.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> What is a Default Room Configuration?

A recommended equipment setup defined by your organisation admin, based on room size and usage type. When you design a room in BMR Studio that matches a configuration's size range, the recommendations appear in the **Guidelines** panel — showing which categories are already fulfilled and which are still missing, with a **Place in room** action to add any missing items directly. See [Default Room Configuration](../lifecycle/design/default-room-configuration) for details.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Can I add my own items to the catalog?

The **Equipment Catalog** is managed by the **BMR team** — you don't add or edit items yourself. To request a new item, use [Send Feedback](send-feedback) with its **name**, **brand**, and **SKU** (plus a datasheet or product link if you have one). See [Requesting a Catalog Item](../catalog/requesting-items) for exactly what's needed by category.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> What is Maintenance?

Maintenance uses the **Active Design** — the live record of the room as installed — as a single source of truth that **IT, facilities, and support teams** can all work from, without manual upkeep. Live monitoring, troubleshooting workflows, and upgrade requests are on the roadmap.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> How do I move a room to a different organisation or location?

Open the room and go to the **Overview** page. Tap **Edit** to enter edit mode — you can then select a new organisation and location for the room. Save your changes to apply the move.

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> How do I delete a location?

Open the **Organisation** page and go to the **Locations** tab. Each location entry has a **garbage bin** icon — tap it to delete that location.

### <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Platform Limitations

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Do you support Android devices?

No. The app runs only on iOS devices with LiDAR. The BMR web platform works in any modern browser — see [System Requirements](system-requirements).

### <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> Support

#### <Icon name="circle-question-mark" size="1em" /> Who do I contact if I get stuck?

Use **[Send Feedback](send-feedback)** to send a Bug report, Feature request, Question, or Other message directly to the BMR team. It is in the **header** on the Web (labelled *Report an Issue*) and under **Settings** in BMR Mobile (labelled *Send Feedback*). The team monitors this channel actively, so it is the fastest way to get a reply.
