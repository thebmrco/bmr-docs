# <Icon name="users" size="1em" /> Inviting Users to an Organisation

<PageIntro illustration="invite" meta={['Web platform', 'Admins']}>

Invite your team from the organisation's **Users** tab. They get an email, set a password, and land in your organisation with the role you chose.

## <Icon name="key" size="1em" /> Roles

Three roles. Pick the narrowest one that fits.

| Role | Scope | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Whole organisation | Everything — all locations and rooms, users, organisation settings. |
| **Location Manager** | One location | The rooms and data in that location. No users, no organisation settings. |
| **Room Manager** | One room | That room — scans, markers, measurements and designs. |

See [Structure](../structure/#-what-each-level-is-for) for how roles follow the levels.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> How to invite someone

### 1. Open the Organisation page and go to the Users tab

On the web, open your organisation and click **Users**. You see everyone with access, their **Role**, their **Target** (organisation, location or room) and whether they are **Active** or **Invited**.

<Figure
  src="/img/inviting-users/01-users-tab.png"
  alt="Users tab on the Organisation page showing the existing admin and an empty invite row at the bottom"
  caption="The Users tab, with the invite row at the bottom."
/>

### 2. Enter the email and pick a role

In the row at the bottom, type the **email address**, then pick **Admin**, **Location Manager** or **Room Manager**.

<Figure
  src="/img/inviting-users/02-role-dropdown.png"
  alt="Role dropdown open showing Admin, Location Manager, and Room Manager options"
  caption="The three roles."
/>

### 3. Choose the target

Once you pick a role, a **target** dropdown appears:

| Role | Target |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | This organisation (filled in for you) |
| **Location Manager** | A location |
| **Room Manager** | A room |

When there is only one choice, it is filled in for you. Click **Add**.

<Figure
  src="/img/inviting-users/03-target-selected.png"
  alt="Role set to Admin and target dropdown showing the organisation, with the Add button ready"
  caption="Role and target set — click Add."
/>

### 4. Confirm the email address

New to BETTERMEETINGROOMS? The **Invite New User** dialog opens and shows the role you are giving. Type the address again in the second field. **Send Invitation** only works when both match.

Already a BMR user? A **Confirm role change** dialog asks you to confirm the role instead. No email is needed.

<FigurePair
  a={{src: '/img/inviting-users/04-confirm-modal.png', alt: 'Invite New User dialog with only the first email field pre-filled and Send Invitation disabled', label: 'Not confirmed'}}
  b={{src: '/img/inviting-users/05-confirm-modal-filled.png', alt: 'Invite New User dialog with both email fields matching and Send Invitation enabled', label: 'Both match'}}
  caption="Send Invitation unlocks only when the two addresses match."
/>

### 5. Send the invitation

Click **Send Invitation**. The user appears as **Invited** (orange) and turns **Active** (green) once they have signed up.

<Figure
  src="/img/inviting-users/06-invited-status.png"
  alt="Users tab now showing the newly invited user with status Invited alongside the existing Active admin"
  caption="Invited until they sign up."
/>

:::caution Match the address they will sign up with
The invitation is bound to the exact address. If they sign up with another one (`firstname.lastname@` vs `firstname@`), they get a separate account and won't see your rooms.
:::

## <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> What the invitee sees

An email with a **Set Your Password** button. They set a password, then log in on **BMR Mobile** or the **Web** and see what you gave them access to. Their side: [Account Setup](account-setup#by-invitation).

:::info No invitation needed to get started
People can also sign up on their own and create their own organisation. Invite them when they should work in **your** organisation — see [Start your own](account-setup#create-your-own).
:::

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Managing existing users

The **Actions** column on the Users tab:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| <Icon name="mail" size="1em" /> **Resend Invite** | Sends the invitation email again. Only shown for users who have never logged in. |
| <Icon name="delete" size="1em" /> **Remove** | Removes this role. A **Confirm role change** dialog asks first. For an invited user, this cancels the invitation. |

To change someone's role, remove the old one and add the new one.
