# <Icon name="room" size="1em" /> Room

:::tip The room is the point
BETTERMEETINGROOMS is named after what it is for. The [organisation and location](./) exist to hold rooms and report on them — but the **room** is the subject, and the only level where anything is actually captured.
:::

A room belongs to a [location](./location), and holds everything the [Discovery](../lifecycle/discovery/) and Design phases produce:

| What a room holds | Captured by |
|---|---|
| **3D scan**, compass orientation and [AR World Map](../technology/ar-world-map) | [Room Scan](../lifecycle/discovery/room-scan) |
| **Markers** — photos of outlets, AV, constraints | [Markers](../lifecycle/discovery/markers) |
| **Acoustic measurements** — RT60 and MOS | [Acoustic Measurement](../acoustics/acoustics-guide) |
| **Designs** — AR designs and designs from BMR Studio | [Design](../lifecycle/design/) |

---

## <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> How a room's data is linked

The pieces above are not just a list — each one records **what it belongs to**, so you can see at a glance how a room's data fits together.

- Every scan, marker, acoustic measurement and design shows a **Linked to** or **Built on** label — for example *Scan 12 Sep*, *via* a scan, *Custom room*, or *Not linked*.
- A design built from a scan shows **which scan**, and how many markers and measurements that scan has.
- An item that is not linked can be connected at any time with **Link to a scan**.
- The same labels appear on the **Designs**, **Scans** and **Acoustic Measurements** pages, not only inside the room.

:::tip Link what you capture
A design built on a scan carries the room's real geometry, its marker photos and its acoustic results with it. An unlinked item still holds its own data, but it is not tied to the rest of the room's record — so link it to a scan when you can.
:::

## <Icon name="layout" size="1em" /> The room page

Every room has a home page that brings its data together in one place — on the **Web** as the **Room Overview**, and in **BMR Mobile** as the **Room view**. It is where you check a room's status at a glance and jump into scans, markers, acoustics, and designs.

### Room Overview on the Web

The Room Overview page presents the room's key information up front:

- **Room details, ratings, and capabilities** at a glance.
- The **acoustic score (MOS)** — always shown, using the linked measurement when one is set and otherwise the latest measurement. See [Reading Acoustic Results](../acoustics/reading-acoustic-results).
- An **Active** section that previews the **active Design** together with the **scan, acoustic measurements, and markers** it is based on — so you can see, in one view, the design that represents the room and the data behind it.

### Room view in BMR Mobile

The same room is available in **BMR Mobile**, where recent improvements make it easier to work with a room on-site:

- **Newly created rooms are shown at the top** of the room list, so a room you just made is easy to find.
- The **active Design** and the **linked scan, markers, and acoustic measurement** are clearly marked, so you can tell at a glance what the room is built from.
- **Designs can be deleted** directly from inside the Room view.
- A **web-only message** is shown for [VRC designs](../integrations/cisco-vrc), which are edited on the Web.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Moving a room

To move a room to a different organisation or location, open the Room Overview page, tap **Edit**, choose the new organisation and location, and save. See the [FAQ](../getting-started/faq) for a quick reference.
