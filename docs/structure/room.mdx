## <Icon name="layout-panel-left" size="1em" /> Four tabs

| Tab | What you find |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The room at a glance, above. Below the Active row: **Notes**, **Tasks**, **Recent Activity** |
| **Room Data** | Everything captured, newest first. Filter by type. Tick two scans or two measurements to **Compare** |
| **Designs** | All designs and **New Design**. **Activate** one, then **Mark verified** once checked on site |
| **Documents** | **Inventory**: what is in the room. **BOM**: what each design needs. See [Room Inventory](../lifecycle/discovery/inventory) |

## <Icon name="link" size="1em" /> Active: what the design is built on {#-how-a-rooms-data-is-linked}

The **Active** row shows the room's record: the active design and the **scan, measurement and markers** it is built on. Each card says **Linked**.

The same link shows everywhere as **Built on** or **Linked to**. Not linked? **Link to a scan**.

<Figure
  src="/img/release/2026-10-built-on.png"
  alt="The Designs list with a Built on column showing, for each design, the scan it was built on and how many markers and measurements that scan has"
  caption="Built on: the scan behind each design, with its markers and measurements."
/>

Linked to a scan, a design carries the room's real shape, marker photos and acoustics with it.

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Moving a room

**Pencil** next to **Where** → pick the location and floor → save.

- Within the **same organisation** only.
- You need edit rights on the room, and to be Location Manager of the new location, or Admin.

---

## <Icon name="delete" size="1em" /> Deleting a room {#deleting-a-room}

The **bin** at the top right. Location Managers and Admins can delete rooms.

The room goes to the trash. Restoring it from **Trash** is a <FeatureToggleTag /> feature: without it, there is no way back.
