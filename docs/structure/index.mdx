## <Icon name="room" size="1em" /> Everything is captured in a room {#-everything-is-captured-in-a-room}

Scans, markers, acoustics, designs and documents all belong to a **room**. Locations and organisations have no data of their own: they **group** rooms and **summarise** them.

Not sure where something belongs? Almost always: the room.

---

## <Icon name="key" size="1em" /> Roles {#roles}

A role is given **on one level** and covers everything inside it. Pick the narrowest one that fits.

<RolesMatrix />

How to give someone a role: [Inviting Users](../getting-started/inviting-users).

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> What each level gives you {#-what-each-level-is-for}

| | Organisation | Location | Room |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Access** | Admins | Location Managers | Room Managers |
| **Settings** | Set once, applies to every room: acoustics, catalogs, branding, integrations | Address, floors | Type, people, usage |
| **Overview** | Data Completeness and Recent Activity, all locations | Data Completeness and Recent Activity, its rooms | Everything captured, and the active design |
