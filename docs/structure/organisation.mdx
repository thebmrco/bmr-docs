## <Icon name="admin-settings" size="1em" /> The admin area {#-the-admin-area}

Open the organisation on the web. Groups along the top, tabs beneath. Only an **Admin** can change things here.

| Group | Tabs | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | — | Counts, Data Completeness, Recent Activity and the location list |
| **Users** | — | Who has access, and with which role — see [Inviting Users](../getting-started/inviting-users) |
| **Directory** | Locations · Rooms | The [locations](./location) and [rooms](./room) you can reach in the organisation |
| **Preferences** | General · Acoustic · Catalog · Scan models · Bundles <FeatureToggleTag /> · Branding | Organisation-wide settings — [below](#-preferences) |
| **Integrations** | Webex Sync · Microsoft 365 | [Webex Control Hub Sync](../integrations/webex-control-hub-sync) and [Microsoft 365](../integrations/microsoft-365) |
| **Room Configurations** | Yours · Shared with you · BMR Defaults | Recommended equipment per room size — [below](#room-configurations) |

The **Directory** shows what you can reach. With a role only on a room, you see that room, and its location as plain text, with no link and no menu.

## <Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> Preferences {#-preferences}

### General {#general}

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Organization ID** | The identifier to quote when you contact support |
| **Mesh upload** <FeatureToggleTag /> | Under **Capture app**. On: BMR Mobile uploads the textured 3D mesh with every scan. Off: the room geometry only. See [Mesh upload](../lifecycle/discovery/room-scan#mesh-upload-setting) |
| **Automatic design from scans** | Under **Capture app**. On: every new room scan makes a furnished design and makes it the room's **Active** design, in place of the current one. Off (the default): a design is made only with **Turn into Design** or **New Design**. A scan that arrives while it is off never gets one, even if you turn it on later. See [Starting a design](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#starting-a-design) |
| **Cat figures** · **Monster figures** | Offer cats or friendly monsters as figure styles for the chairs in BMR Studio |
| **Cisco room import** | Offer a Cisco Workspace Designer or Video Room Calculator file as the basis of a new design. Off until an Admin turns it on. See [Import a Cisco room](../integrations/cisco-vrc#import-a-cisco-room) |
| **Trash retention** <FeatureToggleTag /> | How long deleted rooms, scans, designs and acoustics can be restored |
| **Delete this organization** | Moves the organisation, its locations, rooms and everything in them to the Trash, all at once |

:::danger Deleting an organisation
The dialog first shows what goes with it: how many **locations**, **rooms**, **designs**, **scans** and **markers**, and how many **users lose access**. Click the users to see who. You type the organisation's name to confirm. The other Admins get an email.

Every role in the organisation is removed: on the organisation, on its locations and on its rooms. So **only the person who deleted it, or BMR, can restore it**, from the **Trash**. Whoever restores it becomes its Admin. Restoring brings back everything that went with it, and leaves alone what was in the Trash before.

Restoring is a <FeatureToggleTag /> feature. Without it, there is no way back in the app.
:::

### Acoustic {#acoustic}

Defaults for new measurements. Earlier ones don't change.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Default measurement mode** | **Advanced** or **Basic** — see [Acoustic Measurement](../acoustics/acoustics-guide) |
| **Target MOS** | The score the organisation aims for, from 1.0 to 5.0. Data analysis views flag rooms below it |
| **Default room usage type** | The DIN 18041 usage class pre-selected when a room is created |
| **Default acoustics catalog** | The catalog whose acoustic items acoustic measurement uses |

:::tip Which measurement mode is used?
The device setting in BMR Mobile wins (**Settings → Acoustic Settings**). Then this default. Then **Advanced**.
:::

### Catalog {#catalog}

The catalogs you can use, with their item counts. BETTERMEETINGROOMS catalogs are **view only**; your own can be managed here. See [Catalog & 3D Models](../catalog/).

### Scan models {#scan-models}

A room scan finds the chairs, tables, cupboards, sofas and displays in the room, and a design made from it places a model for each. **Scan models** says which model, for each kind of object.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kinds** | **Chair**, **Swivel chair**, **Stool**, **Rectangular table**, **Round or oval table**, **Cupboard**, **Shelf**, **Sofa** and **Display** |
| **Generic model** | What a kind uses until you choose one |
| **Choose…** | Pick an item from the catalog. Only items with a 3D model are offered |
| **Use generic** | Goes back to the generic model |
| **Fallbacks** | With no model of its own, a swivel chair or a stool takes the Chair's, a round table the Rectangular table's, a shelf the Cupboard's |

Only an **Admin** can change them. A display left on generic becomes a screen scaled to the size in the scan. See [Starting a design](../lifecycle/design/bmr-studio#starting-a-design).

## <Icon name="sliders-horizontal" size="1em" /> Room Configurations {#room-configurations}

Recommended equipment per room size, suggested when you design a room.

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Yours** | Your organisation's own configurations — **Add a room configuration** to make one |
| **Shared with you** | Configurations other organisations shared with yours |
| **BMR Defaults** | Configurations from BETTERMEETINGROOMS, offered to every organisation |

Fully documented in **[Default Room Configuration](../lifecycle/design/default-room-configuration)**.
