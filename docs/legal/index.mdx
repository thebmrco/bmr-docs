## <Icon name="terms-of-service" size="1em" /> Terms versions {#versions}

Every published version of the Terms stays available here.

- Each version has a number (1.0, 1.1, 2.0), a publication date and an effective date.
- Changes that only fix wording or are in your favour are published with notice. Changes that matter for your rights, price or data use are sent to your Organisation Admins at least 6 weeks before they take effect, with a summary (Terms 11.1).
- We keep a record of who accepted which version and when (Terms 2.6).
