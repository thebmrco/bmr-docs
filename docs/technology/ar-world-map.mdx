# <Icon name="target" size="1em" /> AR World Map

A room's scan, markers, acoustic measurements and AR designs are often captured on different visits. To line up in 3D they share **one coordinate system**. Before you add anything new, BMR Mobile **recognises the room** to find it again.

:::info AR World Map — "AR Map" for short
The **AR World Map** is the set of visual feature points that lets a device recognise a room it has seen before. It is created by the **room scan** — no scan, no AR Map. BMR Mobile shows it as **AR map** on the room's Overview.
:::

---

## <Icon name="room" size="1em" /> One coordinate space per room

**Everything starts with the [room scan](../lifecycle/discovery/room-scan).** The first LiDAR scan of a room does two things at once:

1. It builds the **3D model** of the room.
2. It establishes the room's **coordinate space** — an origin and axes — and saves the **AR World Map**: the visual feature points a device uses to recognise the room on a later visit.

Both come from that one scan. Until a room has been scanned it has no coordinate space and no AR Map, so nothing captured in it can be positioned in 3D.

Everything captured afterwards is **anchored to that same space**. A marker's `x, y, z`, an acoustic measurement's position, and an AR-placed display all refer to the same origin — which is exactly why they can be shown together, compared, and combined in BMR Studio.

## <Icon name="scan" size="1em" /> Why the app detects the room first

A new **AR Design**, **marker** or **acoustic measurement** starts with the device not knowing where it is. It matches the camera view to the AR Map's feature points (*relocalization*) and recovers the room's coordinates. Without that match, new data can't be placed in 3D.

### What you see

| On screen | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Recognising this room** · **Look around the room** | Matching the live camera to the saved map. |
| **% of room seen** | How much of the room you have shown it. |
| **Keep turning left** / **right** | Turn that way. |
| **Room recognised** | Match found. You carry on (for a measurement, tap **Continue**). |
| **Skip positioning** | Carry on without a match: see [below](#-if-it-still-cant-find-the-room). |

## <Icon name="alert-triangle" size="1em" /> Why detection sometimes fails

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> Getting a reliable match

- **Start where the scan started**, facing the same way.
- **Aim at detail**: corners, furniture, posters. Not blank walls, glass or mirrors.
- **Turn slowly and steadily**, and follow **Keep turning left / right**.
- **Match the lighting**: same blinds, same lights.

---

## <Icon name="info" size="1em" /> If it still can't find the room

After about 20 seconds without a match: **Still can't recognise this room**.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Try again** | Starts matching again. Move to a part of the room with more detail first. |
| **Continue without positioning** | Carries on with no coordinates (same as **Skip positioning**). |
| **Re-scan the room** | Closes the flow. Scan the room again if it has changed (renovation, new furniture, repaint). |
| **Learn why this happens** | Opens the BMR docs. |

Without positioning:

| Capture | Result |
|---|---|
| **AR Design** | Limited mode: only photos upload, as markers without coordinates. Placed items are not uploaded. |
| **Marker** | Saved as a photo without coordinates. |
| **Acoustic measurement** | **Position Tracking Skipped**: measured without device position. |

## <Icon name="puzzle" size="1em" /> Where this applies

Room detection runs whenever new spatial data is added to a scanned room, so it stays aligned with everything already captured:

- Starting an **[AR Design](../lifecycle/design/ar-design-guide)**
- Adding a **marker**
- Running an **acoustic measurement**
