# <Icon name="organization" size="1em" /> Organisation structure

Everything in BETTERMEETINGROOMS lives in a three-level hierarchy. Each level contains the next, and data captured at the bottom rolls up to the top.

**Organisation → Location → Room**

| Level | What it is | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Organisation** | Your company or business unit — the top level, and the boundary for access and settings | Locations, users and roles, organisation-wide preferences, catalogs, integrations |
| **Location** | A physical site: an office, a building, a campus | The rooms at that site, and its address |
| **Room** | A single meeting room | Scans, markers, acoustic measurements, designs — everything from Discovery onwards |

An organisation is created for you when you are invited into one, or by the onboarding wizard when you [start your own](../getting-started/account-setup). Most companies need only one.

---

## <Icon name="circle-check" size="1em" /> What rolls up

Room data is captured in the room, but it is summarised at the organisation level so you can see progress across the estate without opening rooms one by one.

- **Data Completeness** — the share of rooms that have been scanned, have markers, have an acoustic measurement, and have a design. Each row links through to the rooms behind it.
- **Recent Activity** — the latest changes across every location, with who made them and when.
- **Counts** — locations, rooms, and users with active roles.

These are views of the same room data described in [Discovery](../lifecycle/discovery/), not a separate record.

---

## <Icon name="permissions" size="1em" /> Access follows the hierarchy

Roles are scoped to a level, and a role grants access to everything inside it:

| Role | Sees and manages |
|---|---|
| **Org Admin** | The whole organisation — every location and room, plus settings and users |
| **Location Manager** | One location and the rooms inside it |
| **Room Manager** | A single room |

No role ever exposes data outside its level. See [Inviting Users](../getting-started/inviting-users) for how roles are assigned.

---

## <Icon name="admin-settings" size="1em" /> Set once, applies everywhere

Anything configured at the organisation level applies to every location and room beneath it — measurement defaults, the equipment catalogs available, branding, and integrations.

See **[Organisation settings](./settings)** for what can be configured and where.
